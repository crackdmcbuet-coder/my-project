## 1. IslamQA (Shaykh Muhammad Salih al-Munajjid)

### Takeaway
IslamQA's line is conditional permission. Listening to a recording is recommended (mustahabb), and attentive listening outside prayer and the khutbah is not obligatory, which IslamQA calls the majority view. Playing it while working is allowed if one listens "as much as one can." It is discouraged when nobody is listening, when people are talking or arguing over it, or when the place is inappropriate. For those cases the advice is to turn it off. IslamQA quotes both the lenient (al-Fawzan) and the stricter (Ibn Uthaymeen) views.

### Cited Findings
- **Fatwa 103137, "استماع القرآن مع الانشغال بالعمل" (Listening to the Quran while busy working).** Per the snippet, listening to the Quran via radio or recorder is a righteous act that earns reward ("عمل صالح يؤجر عليه"). There is no harm in doing so while working, provided the person listens as much as possible. Busying oneself with something that makes listening impossible is discouraged as contrary to the etiquette of honouring the Quran. — [IslamQA 103137](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/103137)
  - The answer quotes **Salih al-Fawzan**, asked about listening while cooking. He said there is no problem, because insat (attentive silence) is required only according to ability: the busy person listens as far as he can. — [IslamQA 103137](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/103137); English translation of al-Fawzan's answer: [darussaafi (al-Fawzan)](https://darussaafi.wordpress.com/2015/11/29/listening-to-the-quran-while-working-shaikh-salih-al-fawzaan/)
  - The answer also quotes **Ibn Uthaymeen** (Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh): it is not good etiquette to play the Book of Allah, even from a tape, while you are heedless of it. His words: **"إن كنت متفرغاً لاستماعه فاستمع، وإن كنت مشغولاً فلا تفتحه"** ("If you are free to listen, listen; if you are busy, don't turn it on."). He allowed it for someone lying down with nothing else to do who listens until he falls asleep. — [IslamQA 103137](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/103137); same text in [Islamweb 146263](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/146263/)
- **Fatwa 88728, "When is it obligatory to listen attentively to recitation of the Quran?"** Scholars differ. The view IslamQA favours is that attentive listening is **obligatory in prayer and during the Friday khutbah, and mustahabb elsewhere**. Listening to Quran on radio or tapes while working is fine, and the person who plays the tape should listen as much as he can. Calling it mustahabb "does not mean that one may be careless about listening when Quran is recited." — [IslamQA 88728 (EN)](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/88728); [Arabic](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/88728/متى-يجب-الانصات-لتلاوة-القران)
  - FLAG: one search summary attributed the "fine while working, listen as much as he can" statement to Ibn Uthaymeen inside 88728. That sits uneasily with his "if busy, don't turn it on" quote above. It may be al-Fawzan's or the Lajnah's wording. **Verify on the page.**
- **Fatwa 185848** (learning recitation with an electronic pen) restates the same point. Attentive listening outside prayer, whether to a live reciter or a recorder, is not obligatory but is mustahabb and prescribed. — [IslamQA 185848](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/185848/ruling-on-learning-recitation-and-tajweed-of-quran-by-means-of-an-electronic-pen)
- **Fatwa 174743, "Leaving a recording of the Quran playing without listening to it."** It calls the not-obligatory view "the more sound of the two views, and the view of the majority of scholars." It allows a recording to play in the background **if nothing drowns it out, there is no noise or idle talk (laghat), and the place is not inappropriate**. If people nearby are chatting or arguing, the radio should be turned off, because that shows disrespect to the Quran. Turning it off is "more appropriate" in some cases. — [IslamQA 174743](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/174743/leaving-a-recording-of-the-quran-playing-without-listening-to-it)
- **Fatwa 26285**, "كان يستمع إلى القرآن عندما دق جرس الهاتف" (the phone rang while he was listening to Quran). Quotes Ibn Uthaymeen: it is fine to switch off the recorder when you want to talk or stop listening, so that you are not heedless while it plays. — [IslamQA 26285](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/26285)
- **Fatwa 286914:** it is not allowed to play a radio Quran broadcast in the mosque between the adhan and iqamah. Reasons: it disturbs those praying and reciting, and the practice is described as an innovation. — [IslamQA 286914](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/286914)
- **Fatwas 142880 / 2297:** car speakers at passengers' feet. Ibn Uthaymeen (Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh) said playing Quran through speakers placed at foot or shoe level is disrespectful, and the speaker should be moved up. — [IslamQA 142880](https://islamqa.info/ar/142880); [IslamQA 2297](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/2297)

### Inferences
- IslamQA's own position is the lenient-with-etiquette one: recommended, not obligatory. The real prohibitions are laghat (idle talk) and disrespect. It presents Ibn Uthaymeen's stricter advice as a matter of adab (etiquette), not a declaration of sin.
- No IslamQA fatwa that I found addresses "reading a book while Quran plays" by name. The relevant category is 103137's "work that makes listening impossible." Reading a book plausibly falls there, since one cannot follow two streams of language at once. That is my inference.

### Gaps
- Could not open the full text of 103137, 88728 or 174743. The exact Arabic wording and the source citations they give for al-Fawzan and Ibn Uthaymeen (volume and page) are unverified.

## 2. Ibn Baz, Ibn Uthaymeen, al-Lajnah al-Da'imah, al-Fawzan

### Takeaway
- **Ibn Baz:** what is prescribed is listening. If busy, it is better to turn the radio off. Light, silent work such as laundry is fine even if some recitation is missed. Work that needs thought should not be done with it playing. **Talking over it or reading something else while it plays is not right: "either listen attentively or switch it off."** Laghat (idle chatter) is the clearest prohibition.
- **Ibn Uthaymeen:** the strictest. "If busy, don't turn it on." It is poor etiquette (adab) to play it while heedless. He allowed it when lying down to sleep.
- **Lajnah Da'imah:** playing a recorder loudly in an office is not permitted if it makes employees neglect work, fails to bring about listening, or disturbs others. Whoever turns it on should listen or turn it off.
- **al-Fawzan:** the most lenient. No harm; listen as far as you are able.

### Cited Findings
**Ibn Baz (binbaz.org.sa)**
- **Fatwa 14407, "حكم الانشغال أثناء سماع القرآن"** (ruling on being busy while hearing Quran). A woman leaves the Quran radio station on while working and does not really listen. Answer as reported: what is prescribed is listening and insat. If work prevents that, **it is better to switch the radio off until there is an opportunity to listen**. As for someone fiddling about near the Quran or chattering idly while it is recited before them: "**أما كونه يعبث عند القرآن، ويتكلم بالفضول والقرآن بين أيديهم، لا**" ("As for someone fooling around near the Quran and indulging in idle talk while it is being recited before them: no."). Also: "**إذا كان الشغل ما فيه لغط ولا كلام إنما شغل مع سكوت وتستمعين، وقد يفوتك بعض الشيء فهذا لا بأس به**" ("If the work involves no noise or talk, only quiet work while you listen, and you miss a little, that is fine."), citing 7:204. — [binbaz 14407](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/14407/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%BA%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86); mirror [e-quran.com](https://e-quran.com/qa/ar/fatwa/23d5d3326e4cf9b0/)
- **Fatwa 8673, "حكم الاستماع إلى القرآن حال التفكير وعمل المطبخ والغسيل"** (listening while thinking, cooking or doing laundry). Laundry is easy and listening is possible alongside it, "**لكن المقصود الشيء الذي يحتاج إلى فكر، إذا كان يحتاج إلى فكر وتأمل يغلق المذياع**" ("What matters is work that needs thought: if it needs thought and reflection, he should switch off the radio."). **This is the closest Salafi text to the "reading or studying while Quran plays" question:** mental work equals switch it off. — [binbaz 8673](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8673/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B7%D8%A8%D8%AE-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%84)
- **Fatwa 10596, "حكم استماع المرأة القرآن أثناء تأدية أعمال المنزل"** (a woman listening to Quran while doing housework). "**سماع القرآن كله خير**" ("Listening to the Quran is all good."). If she turns on the radio and listens while doing some chores, she has combined two benefits, and there is no sin on her. — [binbaz 10596](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10596/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B2%D9%84); [al-fatawa.com mirror](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/78037/)
- **Fatwa 18065, "حكم الكلام أو القراءة مع الاستماع لإذاعة القرآن الكريم"** (talking **or reading** while the Quran radio plays). If the station is broadcasting news or other material, talking is fine. If it is broadcasting Quran: "**فإما أن ينصت، وإما أن يغلقها، ويشتغل بشغله**" ("Either he listens attentively, or he switches it off and gets on with his work."). **This is the most directly relevant fatwa for the "reading while Quran plays" question.** — [binbaz 18065](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/18065/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%88-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%85)
  - FLAG: the snippet does not show whether "القراءة" (reading) in the title means reciting Quran oneself or reading generally. Compare fatwa 7974, "حكم تلاوة القرآن مع إذاعة القرآن الكريم" (reciting Quran alongside the Quran radio), which treats the recitation case separately. — [binbaz 7974](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/7974/)
- **Fatwa 16145, "حكم تشغيل المسجل بالقرآن والخروج من المنزل"** (playing a Quran recorder and leaving the house). The search summary reports: if people around the device talk idly without need, not playing it is better, because that involves a kind of disrespect (imtihan) to the Quran. If nobody is there, or those present are listening or asleep, there is no harm. — [binbaz 16145](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/16145/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%AC-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B2%D9%84)
- **Fatwa 11037** (Quran over mosque loudspeakers) and **15642** (raising the volume of the mosque's Quran broadcast): not permitted, because it disturbs people and prevents them from reading or praying themselves. Exception: a small known group who agree to listen before the imam arrives. — [binbaz 11037](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/11037/); [binbaz 15642](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/15642/)
- On repeating along with the reciter on the radio: the reward is in listening attentively, not in repeating. — [al-fatawa.com 86064 (Ibn Baz)](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/86064/)

**Ibn Uthaymeen**
- **Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh, no. 146**, "سماع القرآن قبل النوم أو وقت المذاكرة أو وقت الانشغال" (listening to Quran before sleep, **while studying**, or when busy). It is not good etiquette to let the Book of Allah be recited, even from a tape, while one is heedless of it: "**إن كنت متفرغاً لاستماعه فاستمع، وإن كنت مشغولاً فلا تفتحه**" ("If you are free to listen, listen; if you are busy, don't turn it on."). Someone lying down with nothing else to do who listens to fall asleep is fine. **The question explicitly covers study time (المذاكرة).** — [Shamela, Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh p.15 of no.146](https://shamela.ws/book/7687/4121); [al-fatawa.com 128492](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/128492/)
- **al-fatawa.com 43050:** Ibn Uthaymeen asked about playing a Quran recorder while the household is busy with other things. Title only; text not retrieved. — [al-fatawa.com 43050](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/43050/)
- **al-fatawa.com 129611:** Ibn Uthaymeen on the meaning of 7:204. Title only; text not retrieved. — [al-fatawa.com 129611](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/129611/)
- **Car speakers at foot level are disrespectful** (Liqa' al-Bab al-Maftuh). — [IslamQA 2297](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/2297)

**al-Lajnah al-Da'imah (Permanent Committee)**
- **"تشغيل المسجل بالقرآن مع الانشغال عن السماع"** (playing a Quran recorder while too busy to listen). The question came from an office (reportedly a security agency) where a Quran recorder ran almost continuously at high volume. The committee answered that this is **not permitted** if it leads employees to neglect their work, does not get people to actually listen, or disturbs others. — [al-fatawa.com 103766](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/103766/) (fatwa number and volume not visible in the snippet)
- A related Lajnah statement, reported via al-Qabas: whoever turns on a radio or recorder for Quran should listen attentively, and if that is not possible, should switch it off. Being silent is not enough if the mind is occupied with other work. — [alqabas.com](https://alqabas.com/watch/5744687-سماع-القرآن-أثناء-العمل-يتنافى-مع-قدسيته) (secondary press source; no fatwa number)
- **"حكم الإنصات والاستماع للقرآن"** (ruling on listening attentively to Quran, Lajnah). Title only. — [al-fatawa.com 103501](https://al-fatawa.com/fatwa/103501/)
- Recording Quran recitation on tapes is permissible in itself if it is correct. — [Shamela, Fatawa al-Lajnah, 2nd collection, vol. 3 p.118](https://shamela.ws/book/21772/1695)

**Salih al-Fawzan**
- There is no harm in listening to the Quran from a radio or recorder while working, since insat is required only according to one's ability. — quoted in [IslamQA 103137](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/103137); [English translation](https://darussaafi.wordpress.com/2015/11/29/listening-to-the-quran-while-working-shaikh-salih-al-fawzaan/)

### Inferences
- Ibn Baz and Ibn Uthaymeen both treat listening to a recording as falling under the adab of 7:204. Ibn Baz cites 7:204 directly in 14407 and 18065. Neither argues that a recording is "not real qira'ah" and therefore exempt. Their reasoning concerns respect for the Quran and attentive listening, not the technical status of the recording.
- Gradation across these scholars: al-Fawzan permits within one's capacity. Ibn Baz permits light silent work, but says to switch it off for thinking work or talking. Ibn Uthaymeen says to switch it off when busy. The Lajnah says impermissible in an office setting with neglect or disturbance.
- None of these texts, as snippeted, says that leaving it playing while distracted is a major sin. The language is "لا ينبغي" (it is not proper), "الأفضل/الأولى إغلاقه" (it is better to switch it off), "ليس من الأدب" (not good etiquette), "لا" (no) for laghat, and "لا يجوز" (not permitted) only in the Lajnah's office case, which involves neglecting work and disturbing others. These are my readings of the snippet wording.

### Gaps
- I did not find a specific Ibn Uthaymeen fatwa ruling on **whether a recording counts as "qira'ah" for the purposes of 7:204**, or one on the sajdat al-tilawah analogy. There are known Fiqh discussions (for example, on prostration of recitation when hearing a recording), but I could not retrieve them here.
- No Fatawa Nur 'ala al-Darb number was confirmed for the Ibn Baz fatwas. The site pages above are the citable units.
- I did not find a separate al-Fawzan fatwa (with a number from al-Muntaqa) beyond the IslamQA quotation.
- I did not find a fatwa that specifically covers **driving** while Quran plays, other than the car-speaker placement issue.

## 3. Islamweb.net (Qatar Fatwa Centre)

### Takeaway
Islamweb holds that it is **makruh (disliked), not haram**, to play Quran where nobody listens or everyone is busy with something else. That follows the Hanbali fuqaha'. Ibn 'Aqil held it haram because it is disrespect (imtihan). It is fine if at least some of those present listen, or if work does not distract the person from listening. Some Islamweb fatwas are more lenient, for example allowing a tape to run at home even if nobody listens.

### Cited Findings
- **Fatwa 146263, "حكم تشغيل القرآن مع عدم الاستماع إليه"** (playing Quran without listening to it): "**فلا ينبغي تشغيل القرآن الكريم إلا حيث يستمع إلى التلاوة، لأن في تشغيله مع الإعراض عنه وعدم الاستماع إليه إشعاراً بامتهانه**" ("Quran should only be played where the recitation is listened to, because playing it while turning away from it and not listening suggests disrespect."). It quotes Ibn Uthaymeen's "if busy, don't turn it on." If anyone present listens, there is no harm. "**فإن كان جميع من بالمكتب يلهون عن استماع القرآن ويشتغلون بما هم فيه فلا ينبغي تشغيل القرآن والحال هذه**" ("If everyone in the office is distracted from listening and busy with their own work, the Quran should not be played in that situation."). — [Islamweb 146263](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/146263/)
- **Fatwa 160233, "يكره تشغيل القرآن حيث لا يستمع إليه أحد"** (it is disliked to play Quran where nobody listens). It is "**مما لا ينبغي، وهو مكروه كما نص على ذلك فقهاء الحنابلة، ولا يصل إلى حد التحريم**" ("one of the things that should not be done; it is makruh, as the Hanbali jurists stated, and does not reach the level of prohibition"). **Ibn 'Aqil held it prohibited** because of the disrespect to the Quran. — [Islamweb 160233](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/160233/)
- **Fatwa 378760, "حكم تشغيل القرآن مع القيام بالدراسة أو العمل أو الأكل"** (playing Quran while studying, working or eating), dated 22 Shawwal 1439 / 5 July 2018. The proper approach is attentive listening. Playing it while occupied with study or work is discouraged, especially when those present are busy with worldly matters. It refers back to 146263. "**إذا لم يتشاغل الشخص عن السماع بعمله، فلا حرج في ذلك**" ("If the person is not distracted from listening by his work, there is no harm in it."). **This one directly addresses study.** — [Islamweb 378760](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/378760/)
- **Fatwa 5256, "لا حرج في تشغيل الجهاز لقراءة القرآن ولو لم يستمع إليه أحد"** (no harm in playing a device's recitation even if nobody listens). "**لا بأس بتشغيل شريط القرآن في المنزل وإن لم يوجد أحد يستمع إليه**" ("There is no objection to playing a Quran tape at home even if nobody is there to listen."). It cites Ibn Baz on the condition that there is no idle talk or laghat without need. **This is more lenient, and it conflicts in emphasis with 160233.** — [Islamweb 5256](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/5256/)
- **Fatwa 206962, "تشغيل القرآن في مكان لا يُستمع إليه فيه"** (playing Quran in a place where it is not listened to). Title only. — [Islamweb 206962](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/206962/)
- **Fatwa 120635, "سماع القرآن حال الدراسة والتدريس"** (listening to Quran while studying and teaching). Title only. A search summary says scholars differ on a teacher lecturing while Quran plays. — [Islamweb 120635](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/120635/)
- **Fatwa 40027, "الاستماع للقرآن أثناء العمل"** (listening to Quran during work). Per the summary, playing it quietly is still preferable to not listening at all when focus is difficult. — [Islamweb 40027](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/40027/)
- **Fatwa 26180, "ينزه القرآن عن أن يُقرأ في أماكن التسوق"** (the Quran should be kept above being recited in shopping places). Some scholars warn against loud Quran in markets and malls as disrespect, since people are busy buying and selling and cannot listen. — [Islamweb 26180](https://islamweb.net/amp/ar/fatwa/26180)
- **English fatwas:** 85489, "Playing Qur'an tapes at work": no objection if the employer accepts it and the work does not suffer — [Islamweb EN 85489](https://islamweb.net/en/printfatwa.php?id=85489). 81426, "Qur'anic recitation of radio, but no one listening": recommended to turn it off if nobody listens — [Islamweb EN 81426](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/81426/quranic-recitation-of-radio-but-no-one-listening). 91383, "Listening to the Quran while at work" — [Islamweb EN 91383](https://islamweb.net/en/printfatwa.php?id=91383).

### Inferences
- Islamweb's clearest classification, in 160233, is **makruh (Hanbali), not haram, with a minority view (Ibn 'Aqil) of haram**. That is the most useful single citation for "is it a sin?"
- For "reading or studying while Quran plays," 378760 gives the test: is the person actually distracted from listening? If yes, it is discouraged. If not, there is no harm.

### Gaps
- Could not open any Islamweb page. The full texts and the exact citation of Ibn 'Aqil (probably via al-Furu' or al-Adab al-Shar'iyyah) are unverified.

## 4. Sin vs. disliked; "background noise" (الإعراض / التشويش) vs. a reader with Quran in the background

### Takeaway
None of the Salafi or Gulf sources found calls it a major sin. The range is **"not proper / poor etiquette" (Ibn Uthaymeen) → "makruh" (Islamweb, following the Hanbalis) → "not permitted" (Lajnah, when it causes neglect of duty or disturbs others; Ibn Baz, for idle talk over it)**. The decisive factors the scholars name are: (a) i'radh (deliberately turning away from it) or laghat (idle talk over it), (b) tashwish (disturbing others who are praying, reading, sleeping or ill), and (c) the place (feet, markets). Light manual work with partial attention is generally allowed. Mental work (thinking, studying, reading) is where Ibn Baz and Ibn Uthaymeen both say to turn it off.

### Cited Findings
- Ibn Baz: work needing thought means switch it off — [binbaz 8673](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/8673/); talking or reading while Quran plays means "either listen or switch it off" — [binbaz 18065](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/18065/).
- Ibn Uthaymeen: the question explicitly includes study time, and the answer is "if busy, don't turn it on" — [Shamela LBM 146](https://shamela.ws/book/7687/4121).
- Makruh, not haram (Hanbali); Ibn 'Aqil says haram — [Islamweb 160233](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/160233/).
- Disrespect (imtihan) as the rationale — [Islamweb 146263](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/146263/); [binbaz 16145](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/16145/).
- Disturbance (tashwish) of others as the rationale in mosques — [binbaz 11037](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/11037/); [IslamQA 286914](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/286914).
- Reward: listening to a recording is a rewarded righteous deed — [IslamQA 103137](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/103137); "listening to the Quran is all good" — [binbaz 10596](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/10596/).

### Inferences
- For the specific scenario of **reading a book with Quran playing in the background**, the most direct Salafi evidence (Ibn Baz 18065 and 8673, Ibn Uthaymeen LBM 146, Islamweb 378760) points to: **better to switch it off, or to listen. It is not a sin unless there is i'radh, laghat, disrespect, or disturbance of others.** The lenient minority (al-Fawzan; IslamQA's "as much as you can") would tolerate it if some real listening happens.

### Gaps
- No source found explicitly says that leaving it playing earns no reward at all. The sources speak of etiquette and disrespect, not of loss of reward.

## 5. Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya / Al-Azhar

### Takeaway
Egypt's Dar al-Ifta is more permissive. Playing the Quran radio "for blessing" even without listening is allowed, subject to conditions: it must not distract from work, must not be loud enough to disturb others, and there must be no deliberate turning away or chatter. It criticises shops that blast Quran stations for hours while people are busy with amusement and do not listen.

### Cited Findings
- **Dar al-Ifta fatwa 15175, "حكم تشغيل القرآن الكريم وعدم الاستماع إليه"** (playing the Quran without listening to it). The Quran itself is not harmed by being recited or heard, but listeners should attend to it and avoid chatter that distracts from it. It criticises shopkeepers who leave loud Quran radio on for long hours, even overnight, harming neighbours while they are "**في لهو ولعب ومرح ولا يستمعون لما فتحوه**" ("in amusement, play and merriment, not listening to what they turned on"). — [Dar al-Ifta 15175](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/15175/)
- **Dar al-Ifta fatwa 13220, "حكم تشغيل القرآن الكريم في أوقات العمل الرسمية"** (playing the Quran during official working hours). Title only. Press reports describe two conditions: it must not distract from work, and it must not disturb others. — [Dar al-Ifta 13220](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/13220/); [Elbalad report](https://www.elbalad.news/5378723)
- **Press reports on Ali Gomaa and Dar al-Ifta:** it is permissible to leave Quran radio on for baraka (blessing) without listening. Elbalad reports "4 conditions." The sin lies in deliberate turning away or distracting listeners. The reports are inconsistent on details (sleeping with it on, and the number of conditions). — [Elbalad 7173508](https://www.elbalad.news/7173508); [Elbalad 7189045](https://www.elbalad.news/7189045); [Elwatan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/6696719)
- **Maspero (Egyptian state radio) answer, May 2026:** playing Quran over loudspeakers in shops and cafés while people ignore it amid the noise of trade, or where it disturbs neighbours and the sick, is "not permissible in Sharia." Incidental distraction for a valid reason is excused, provided one does not completely turn away on purpose. — [Maspero](https://www.maspero.eg/radio/2026/05/23/956765/)
- **IslamOnline (Qaradawi-linked fiqh site; not Salafi):** listening while studying or working is fine even if distracted, because it is not intended as turning away. — [fiqh.islamonline.net](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A2%D9%86-%D8%A3%D8%AB%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A9/)

### Inferences
- The Egyptian and Azhari position is the most permissive. It turns on intent (no deliberate i'radh) and on not harming others, whereas the Saudi Salafi position turns on adab and active listening.

### Gaps
- The exact "4 conditions" of Dar al-Ifta could not be verified from the primary page. They are reported only in the Egyptian press, and the reports disagree with each other.
- No formal Al-Azhar (Majma' al-Buhuth) fatwa was found.
