# Bengali, Urdu and English Islamic book market: a husband between his mother and his wife (মা ও স্ত্রীর মাঝে ভারসাম্য / শাশুড়ি-বউ সম্পর্ক / ماں اور بیوی کے حقوق، ساس بہو / "between mother and wife")

> Method note: rokomari.com, wafilife.com, goodreads.com, cpsglobal.org and dawahbooks.com.pk could not be fetched from this research session (the network blocks those hosts). Details for those pages come from search-engine result snippets, not from reading the pages. Page counts, prices and translator names marked "(snippet)" should be checked on the live page before anyone relies on them. A later pass should open these product pages directly if the hosts are allowed.

## 1. Which Bengali Islamic books on this theme exist?

### Takeaway
The Bengali market has a few short books on the **mother-in-law/daughter-in-law (শাশুড়ি-বউ)** relationship. At least one is a translation from English (Ilham's শাশুড়ি বউমার মেলবন্ধন, almost certainly *When the Two Seas Meet*). I found **no** Bengali book, original or translated, written for the **husband/son** that deals in depth with balancing the rights of his mother and his wife (মা ও স্ত্রীর হকের ভারসাম্য). Only one 32-page Bengali booklet is aimed at the husband. The husband-focused angle is a clear gap.

### Inferences
- **Do not translate *When the Two Seas Meet*.** It already exists in Bengali as Ilham's শাশুড়ি বউমার মেলবন্ধন. The match is strong (same co-authors, same Bengali title theme, a named translator) but rests on author-name matching. Confirm by checking the copyright/credits page of the Ilham edition.
- Existing Bengali titles are written for the **mother-in-law or daughter-in-law**. The only husband-focused title is a 32-page booklet. A full, scholarly, fiqh-grounded book addressed to the **son/husband**, explaining the separate rights of mother and wife, what to do when they conflict, separate housing, and obedience limits, is a gap.
- The many Bengali newspaper and fatwa articles on "মা না স্ত্রী" suggest demand but little book-length supply.

### Gaps
- Could not open Rokomari or Wafilife pages (blocked host). Publisher, year and page counts for শাশুড়ি-বউমার বন্ধন (Shahadat Husain) and the exact publisher of বিতর্কে যখন বউ-শাশুড়ি are unverified.
- Did not confirm whether আদর্শ পরিবার or Zulfiqar Naqshbandi's Bengali translations have dedicated mother-wife chapters, or which Urdu originals they come from.
- Did not systematically check the catalogues of Maktabatul Azhar, Sean, Rahnuma, Muhammad Publication, Kalantor, Somokalin or Ruhama. A search mentioning Kalantor and Ruhama found no on-theme title. A book from those publishers may exist and was not found.
- Possible Bengali editions of Dawat-e-Islami booklets (Maktaba-tul-Madina publishes in Bangla) were not checked.

## 2. Notable Urdu books on maa aur biwi ke huquq / saas bahu

### Takeaway
Urdu has **no well-known standalone book titled "ماں اور بیوی کے حقوق"** among the sources searched. The material sits in (a) spousal-rights books, (b) parents'-rights treatises such as Thanvi's *Huqooq-ul-Walidain*, (c) short *saas-bahu* booklets (Dawat-e-Islami), and (d) chapters in larger family books (Wahiduddin Khan, Muhammad Haneef Abdul Majeed). The best Urdu candidates for translation are the *Tohfa-e-Dulha / Tohfa-e-Dulhan* pair, Wahiduddin Khan's *Kamyab Khandani Zindagi*, and Thanvi's short treatise. None was found to have a Bengali translation.

### Cited Findings
1. **تحفۂ دلہن (Tohfa-e-Dulhan)**: Maulana **Muhammad Haneef Abdul Majeed**; Darul Ishaat Deoband (2000 edition, 255 pages per Rekhta); Bayt al-Ilm Trust Karachi edition (2012, 533 pages per Besturdubooks). The content includes avoiding quarrels with in-laws — [Rekhta](https://www.rekhta.org/ebooks/tohfa-e-dulhan-mohammad-haneef-abdul-majeed-ebooks-1); [Besturdubooks](https://besturdubooks.net/tohfa-e-dulhan/); [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/Tohfa-e-Dulhan-109681); [Maktaba Razi Deoband](https://maktabarazi.com/product/tohfa-dulhan/)
2. **تحفۂ دولہا (Tohfa-e-Dulha)**: same author, the companion volume **for husbands** — [Rekhta](https://www.rekhta.org/ebook-detail/tohfa-e-dulha-mohammad-haneef-abdul-majeed-ebooks); [archive.org combined](https://archive.org/details/tohfa-dolha-dulhan). The English edition of the bride book says the advice to men was published separately as the husband's gift — [Gift for Muslim Bride PDF (Swindon Masjid)](https://www.swindonmasjid.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/AGiftForMuslimBrideByShaykhMuhammadHaneefAbdulMajeed.pdf)

3. **ساس بہو میں صلح کا راز (Saas Bahu mein Sulah ka Raaz)**: by **Al-Madina-tul-Ilmiyah** (Dawat-e-Islami's research department), **Maktaba-tul-Madina**, 2008, **32 pages**, free on the Dawat-e-Islami library. Related: "Saas Aur Bahoo Kay Barey Main Madani Phool" (media) and the article "بہو کو نوکرانی مت سمجھئے" — [Dawat-e-Islami library](https://www.dawateislami.net/bookslibrary/548); [Madani Phool](https://www.dawateislami.net/medialibrary/92039); [article](https://www.dawateislami.net/women-magazine/ur/nai-likhari/saas-bahu). ### Inferences
- *Tohfa-e-Dulha* (for husbands) plus the in-law sections of *Tohfa-e-Dulhan* are the most substantial Deobandi-mainstream Urdu sources and seem untranslated into Bengali. They are long, general marriage manuals, though, not focused books. Translating one in full would bring much off-theme content.
- Because no Urdu monograph covers exactly the "son between mother and wife" theme, a **new Bengali compilation** (Thanvi's *Huqooq-ul-Walidain* plus Taqi Usmani's *Meyan Bivi ke Huqooq* plus fatwa material) or an original work may serve better than translating one Urdu book.
- Mufti Taqi Usmani and Zulfiqar Naqshbandi are already heavily translated in Bangladesh. Their general marriage material is probably already in Bengali (see Section 1 for Naqshbandi), so there is less room there.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm a specific Zulfiqar Ahmad Naqshbandi title on maa/biwi. His marriage books exist in Bengali (Section 1), but their Urdu originals were not mapped.
- Could not confirm whether Thanvi's *Huqooq-ul-Islam*, *Bahishti Zewar* sections or *Islah-e-Inqilab-e-Ummat* have a dedicated mother-versus-wife section. The researcher's understanding is that Bahishti Zewar has been in Bengali for a long time, but this is not verified here.
- Bengali translation status of *Kamyab Khandani Zindagi* was not checked.
- Kitabosunnat.com and Besturdubooks were not searched exhaustively for "saas bahu".

## 3. Notable English Islamic books on the theme

### Takeaway
English has a small but focused set of in-law books from smaller publishers (Mumtaz Raffi and Umm Muhammad Abizer), plus chapters in large IIPH titles such as *The Ideal Muslimah*. *When the Two Seas Meet* is already in Bengali. Its sister volume for daughters-in-law, ***Through the Sweet and Salty Seas***, is the clearest English candidate with no Bengali translation found. Neither book is mainly addressed to the husband, and I found no English book written for the husband/son in the middle.

### Cited Findings
1. ***When the Two Seas Meet: Building Bonds – Excelling in the Role of Mother-in-Law***: Mumtaz Raffi (with Umm Muhammad Abizer). Quran- and Sunnah-based reminders for mothers-in-law — [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/55774707-when-the-two-seas-meet); [Happy Muslim Mom](https://happymuslimmom.com/books-to-read/). **Bengali: probably already translated** as শাশুড়ি বউমার মেলবন্ধন (Ilham, translator Maryam Sharmin) — see Section 1.
2. ***Through the Sweet and Salty Seas: An Islamic Guide for the Daughter-in-Law***: **Umm Muhammad Abizer**. Publisher reported as Thabaat Publications (from a retailer snippet, unconfirmed); Goodreads rating about 4.69 from 36 ratings (snippet) — [Dawah Books PK](https://dawahbooks.com.pk/products/through-the-sweet-and-salty-seas-an-islamic-guide-for-the-daughter-in-law); [Goodreads author page](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/23133031.Umm_Muhammad_Abizer). **Bengali translation: none found.** Recent and in copyright (the author is living), so rights would have to be bought from the author or publisher.
3. ***The Ideal Muslimah***: Dr. Muhammad Ali al-Hashimi, translated from Arabic (*Shakhsiyyat al-Mar'ah al-Muslimah*) by Nasiruddin al-Khattab; **IIPH**; about 538–549 pages. It has chapters "The Muslim Woman and her Sons- and Daughters-in-Law" and on treating in-laws, including the wise mother-in-law being fair even when her son is at fault — [islambasics chapter](https://islambasics.com/chapter/the-muslim-woman-and-her-sons-and-daughters-in-law/); [Dar-us-Salam listing](https://dar-us-salam.com/english/women-family/rp12-ideal-muslimah-muslim-woman.html); [Noor Book Store](https://noorbookstore.com/product-details/The-Ideal-Muslimah-IIPH003). An Islamland page in its Bengali section lists the book, but it could not be confirmed as a Bengali translation — [Islamland (ben)](https://www.islamland.com/ben/books/the-ideal-muslimah-1432798900). The researcher believes, without verifying it here, that Bengali translations of al-Hashimi's book exist (e.g., titled আদর্শ মুসলিম নারী). Check before considering it.
4. ***A Gift for the Muslim Bride*** and ***A Gift for the Muslim Groom***: English translations of Muhammad Haneef Abdul Majeed's *Tohfa-e-Dulhan / Tohfa-e-Dulha*. The bride volume covers living in harmony with in-laws and problems with mothers-in-law. One English edition of the bride volume was translated by Mufti Afzal Hussain Ilyas (Adam Publishers, New Delhi, 2004). Another is published by Idara Isha'at-e-Diniyat — [archive.org bride](https://archive.org/download/EnglishislamicBooks_MAE/158GiftForMuslimBride.pdf); [archive.org groom](https://archive.org/download/EnglishislamicBooks_MAE/159GiftForMuslimGroom.pdf); [UiTM catalogue](https://search.library.uitm.edu.my/Record/wils-337125/Details); [Exotic India](https://exoticindia.com/book/details/gift-for-muslim-bride-idg899). For a Bengali translation, translate from the Urdu original, not the English.
5. ***Happily Ever After: A Muslimah's Guide to a Blissful Marriage*** (Uswatul Muslimah): has a section on a daughter-in-law's rights; the listing is marked "No Copyright" — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/873568979/Happily-Ever-After-by-uswatul-muslima). Small, self-published.

## 4. Copyright considerations for each

### Takeaway
Almost every candidate is in copyright. The exceptions are probably Thanvi's works (he died in 1943, so his works are public domain in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh, which use life + 60 years; publisher editions and annotations may still be protected) and items explicitly released openly. Modern English titles need written permission from the author or publisher.

### Cited Findings
- *Mian Biwi: Huqooq aur Zimmedariyan* (Jalili Nadwi) is on archive.org under a **CC BY-ND 4.0** licence. "NoDerivatives" **prohibits translation** without permission — [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/MianBiwiHuqooqAurZimmedariyan)
- *Happily Ever After* (Uswatul Muslimah) is marked "No Copyright" on its listing — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/873568979/Happily-Ever-After-by-uswatul-muslima)
- *Saas Bahu mein Sulah ka Raaz* is freely downloadable from the Dawat-e-Islami library, published by Maktaba-tul-Madina — [Dawat-e-Islami](https://www.dawateislami.net/bookslibrary/548). Free distribution does not equal permission to translate. Dawat-e-Islami runs its own Bengali translation department.
- Thanvi's *Huqooq-ul-Walidain* (22 pages): author Maulana Ashraf Ali Thanvi (d. 1943) — [Rekhta](https://www.rekhta.org/ebooks/detail/huqooq-ul-walidain-maulana-ashraf-ali-thanvi-ebooks)

### Inferences
- **Thanvi (d. 1943):** the original text is likely public domain in Bangladesh (life + 60 years under the Bangladesh Copyright Act 2000; this is the researcher's understanding and not re-verified here). It is safe to translate from an early edition. Avoid copying a modern publisher's footnotes or takhrij.
- **Mufti Taqi Usmani** (living): copyrighted, held by Maktaba Ma'ariful Qur'an / Memon Islamic Publishers. Bangladeshi publishers often translate without formal licences, but permission is advisable.
- **Muhammad Haneef Abdul Majeed (Tohfa-e-Dulha/Dulhan):** his death date was not found. Treat as in copyright, held by Darul Ishaat or Bayt al-Ilm.
- **Wahiduddin Khan** (d. 2021): copyrighted by CPS International/Goodword Books, which actively publish translations themselves.
- **Mumtaz Raffi / Umm Muhammad Abizer** (living): copyrighted. One title is already licensed or translated by Ilham (not confirmed whether licensed).
- **al-Hashimi, *The Ideal Muslimah*:** the Arabic original and the IIPH English translation are both copyrighted. Translate from the Arabic with permission from the author's estate or publisher, not from the IIPH English.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found confirming licensing arrangements for any Bengali edition (e.g., whether Ilham licensed *When the Two Seas Meet*).
- The death date of Muhammad Haneef Abdul Majeed was not established.
- The Bangladesh copyright term was not re-verified from a primary legal source in this session.
