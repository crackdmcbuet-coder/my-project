# Contemporary (20th–21st c.) Arabic Islamic Books on a Man's Mother and His Wife (الزوج بين أمه وزوجته / الحماة والكنة)

> Research notes, researcher sub-task. Searched 2026-11-25.
> **Limits on this research:** archive.org, noor-book.com and saaid.org could not be reached from this session (DNS blocked by the session's network allowlist), and the Google Books API returned HTTP 429. Those sites were seen only through search-engine snippets and could not be browsed or searched directly. Shamela, waqfeya and jamalon were not searched directly either. A follow-up pass with those hosts allowed is strongly recommended before any conclusion that "no such book exists."

## 1. What standalone Arabic Islamic books exist on this exact theme?

### Takeaway
Across about 20 searches with many title variants ("بين الأم والزوجة", "الزوج بين أمه وزوجته", "بين أمي وزوجتي", "أمي وزوجتي", "الحماة والكنة", "بين الحماة والكنة", "فن التعامل مع الحماة", "تعارض حق الوالدين وحق الزوجة"), **I could not verify a single standalone, Sunni, fiqh- or da'wah-oriented Arabic book dedicated specifically to a husband balancing his mother and his wife.** The topic shows up mainly as articles, fatwas, consultations, and chapters inside larger family-fiqh books. The only verified standalone books whose titles name the theme are secular, folkloric or psychological, not Islamic-scholarly works.

### Cited Findings

**A. Verified standalone books with the theme in the title (NOT suitable as Islamic-scholarly sources)**
- **الحماة والكنة: صورة شعبية** (al-Ḥamāh wa-l-Kannah: Ṣūrah Shaʿbiyyah) by **Ṣāliḥ Khraysāt** (صالح خريسات). Published by **Dār Āfāq, 1994, 128 pages**. This is a folkloric and social portrait, not a sharʿī work. The author founded Dār Āfāq in Amman in 1992. Copyright status is unknown, and the author is presumably living or recently living. — [Google Books](https://books.google.de/books/about/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9.html?id=zqrsAAAAIAAJ&redir_esc=y); [jo24 interview with the author](https://jo24.net/post.php?id=1447)
- **الكنة وخوافيها** (al-Kannah wa-Khawāfīhā) by **Samar al-Muṭawwaʿ** (سمر المطوع). Published by **Nova Plus** (نوفا بلس للنشر والتوزيع). The subtitle on Abjjad reads "أسرار علم النفس والعلاقات", so it is a psychology and relationships book, not fiqh. It is a modern commercial title, so copyright permission would be required. — [Abjjad](https://www.abjjad.com/book/2770337794/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AE%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%A7/2771386370)
- noor-book.com has a tag page "الحمو والحماة". Per the search snippet, it lists an Arabic translation of a **Gary Chapman** in-laws book (Western Christian author) and a novel, **إكسورديوم (ماضي الحماة)** by Mervat Ḥusayn al-Ṭanab. Neither is relevant. — [noor-book tag page](https://www.noor-book.com/en/tag/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%88-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9) (snippet only; page could not be fetched)
- Goodreads lists **حماتي ملاك**, described as humorous stories about a woman's problems with her mother-in-law. This is fiction and not relevant. — [search result pointing to Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6336130) (snippet only)
- A book titled **إلى أمي وزوجتي رمزي الحنان** by Dr. Ibrahim al-Shab (AuthorHouse, ISBN 9781491885734) is a dedication-type title. Nothing shows it is a fiqh or balancing guide. — [Amazon](https://us.amazon.com/%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%B2%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%8C%D8%8C%D8%8C-Arabic/dp/1491885734)

**B. Islamic books that contain a dedicated chapter or section on the theme (candidates for excerpting)**
- **عنف المرأة في المجال الأسري** by **Dr. Ḥanān Qarqūtī**, hosted in the Islamweb library. Chapter 2, "عنف المرأة ضد المرأة في المجال الأسري", has a section titled "عنف الحماة مع كنتها". — [Islamweb library](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/1671/5349/%D8%B9%D9%86%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D9%83%D9%86%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7)
- **المسلم وحقوق الآخرين** (on Shamela, book id 96245) states that a wife is not obliged to serve her husband's mother, though doing so is good companionship and noble character ("الزوجة لا يلزمها خدمة أم الزوج ... لكن إن فعلت ذلك فلا شك أنه من حسن العشرة"). Its table of contents includes a section on whether a parent may order a son to divorce his wife. The author was not captured in the snippet. — [Shamela p.47](https://shamela.ws/book/96245/47)
- **فتاوى الشبكة الإسلامية** (Islamweb fatwa compilation on Shamela, id 27107) has headings such as "أم الزوج لها حقوق كثيرة على زوجة ابنها" and "إكرام أم الزوج من حسن التبعل". — [Shamela](https://read.shamela.ws/book/27107/64322)
- **الزواج عند العرب** by ʿAbd al-Salām al-Tarmānīnī is cited by several sites for its analysis of the mother-in-law conflict. This is a historical and social study, not a fiqh manual. — cited in [Alukah, بين الحماة والكنة](https://www.alukah.net/social/0/114672/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9/)

**C. Article series and essays by Islamic writers (short; could be compiled or translated as a risāla with permission)**
- **"بين الحماة والكنة"** by **Dr. ʿAbd al-Ḥakīm al-Anīs** on Alukah. It gathers scholars' comments, calls the conflict largely futile and driven by ignorance and love of control, and notes that it is more common in Aleppo and Hama than in Damascus and Homs. — [Alukah](https://www.alukah.net/social/0/114672/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9/)
- **"سلسلة إصلاح الأسرة (1): علاقة الحماة بكنتها"** on Ṣayd al-Fawāʾid, attributed in a search summary to **Ḥusām al-Dīn al-Kaylānī**. It says most divorces in shariah-court records stem from mother-in-law conflict and argues that the husband ends the problem through wisdom and strength of character. The author attribution is unverified because saaid.org could not be fetched. — [saaid.org](https://saaid.org/mktarat/alzawaj/98.htm)
- **"أزواج وزوجات في قفص الاتهام"** on saaid.org (al-Dhahabi's da'wah page) describes the good husband as one who honours his mother and respects his wife in front of his family. It warns that husbands fuel conflicts, for example by belittling the wife before his mother. — [saaid.org](https://saaid.org/Doat/Althahabi/3.htm) (snippet)
- **"الحماة والكنة: معالجة شرعية"** on Ṣawt al-Salaf (Egyptian Salafi site) gives sharʿī remedies and advice to both mother-in-law and daughter-in-law. — [salafvoice.com](http://www.salafvoice.com/article.aspx?a=4437); a mirror titled "الحماة والكَنة.. معالجة شرعية" is at [islamhub1.wordpress.com](https://islamhub1.wordpress.com/2012/12/31/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%8E%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A9-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9/) (author not verified)
- **"الحماة والكنة بين الوفاق والشقاق"** by **Dr. Aḥmad Rabāḥ** covers psychological and social causes and the wife's right to privacy, with the rule that "a mother-in-law is not a mother, and a daughter-in-law is not a daughter." — [ahmadrabah.com](https://ahmadrabah.com/blogs/mother-in-law-and-daughter-in-law-between-harmony-and-discord/)
- **"موقف الزوج من زوجته مقارنة بأمه وأسرته"** on IslamOnline's fiqh section argues that treating the wife as secondary contradicts mawadda and raḥma. — [fiqh.islamonline.net](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC-%D8%A3%D9%87%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%87/)
- Newspaper columns titled "الحماة والكنّة" by ʿUthmān bin Ḥamad Abā al-Khayl in al-Jazirah (2023) are social commentary. — [al-Jazirah](https://www.al-jazirah.com/2023/20230508/ar4.htm)

### Inferences
- In Arabic Sunni literature this theme is treated mostly in **short, scattered genres** (fatwas, istishārāt, articles, chapters) rather than as a dedicated monograph. A Bengali project may be better served by a **compilation or anthology** (see section 4) than by translating one book.
- The absence of a hit is **not proof of absence.** Small booklets of 30 to 80 pages in this genre often circulate only on noor-book, waqfeya or saaid PDFs, and those sites were unreachable here.

### Gaps
- **No verified standalone Sunni Islamic book was found.** The coordinator should not present any title as an existing book unless a later pass verifies it.
- The following were **not searched directly** because of blocked or unavailable hosts: noor-book.com internal search, waqfeya.net, archive.org full-text, saaid.org book index (saaid.org/book/), shamela.ws title search, islamhouse.com Arabic book index, jamalon and neelwafurat catalogues. Recommended queries for the next pass: `الحماة` / `الكنة` / `أم الزوج` / `أمه وزوجته` / `الأم والزوجة` / `بر الوالدين وحقوق الزوجة` / `التوفيق بين` in title fields.
- Page counts and content outlines could not be confirmed for any Islamic standalone title, because none was verified.

## 2. Availability, free distribution and copyright status

### Takeaway
None of the verified standalone books is free or permitted for redistribution. The Khraysāt (1994) and al-Muṭawwaʿ titles are commercial and secular in focus. The Islamic material that is freely accessible (Alukah, saaid, Islamweb, islamqa, binbaz.org.sa) is mostly by living authors or institutions, so translation and publication would still need permission.

### Cited Findings
- Khraysāt, *الحماة والكنة* (1994): Google Books lists metadata only, with no free full view seen. Copyright is likely in force. — [Google Books](https://books.google.de/books/about/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%A9_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9.html?id=zqrsAAAAIAAJ&redir_esc=y)
- Samar al-Muṭawwaʿ, *الكنة وخوافيها*: sold as an e-book on Abjjad, so it is commercial and copyrighted. — [Abjjad](https://www.abjjad.com/book/2770337794/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AE%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%A7/2771386370)
- Ibn Bāz (d. 1999) and Ibn ʿUthaymīn (d. 2001) fatwas are published by their official foundations, binbaz.org.sa and binothaimeen.net. — [binbaz.org.sa fatwa 7989](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/7989/حكم-طاعة-الام-اذا-امرت-بطلاق-الزوجة)

### Inferences
- For Ibn Bāz and Ibn ʿUthaymīn, the foundations (مؤسسة الشيخ عبد العزيز بن باز الخيرية; مؤسسة الشيخ محمد بن صالح العثيمين الخيرية) hold publication rights. A short translated compilation of fatwas is commonly done, but **formal permission should be sought** for a printed book.
- islamqa.info content (al-Munajjid, living) and Islamweb content (Qatar Awqaf) carry site terms; **permission is needed** for a commercial print edition.
- Alukah articles are by living authors, so **permission is needed.**

### Gaps
- No "طبع لمن أراد" (free-to-print) notice was found on any item, because no qualifying standalone booklet was located.

## 4. Contemporary scholars' fatwas, consultations and lectures that could be compiled

### Takeaway
There is rich, citable fatwa material from Ibn Bāz, Ibn ʿUthaymīn (via islamqa), al-Munajjid / islamqa, Islamweb, Alukah consultations and islamway. A Bengali compilation (جمع وترتيب) organised by sub-issue is the most realistic "book" on this theme.

### Cited Findings

**Core ruling: a mother or father ordering the son to divorce his wife**
- **Ibn Bāz:** if the wife has not harmed the mother and poses no threat to the son's religion, self or property, the mother has no right to separate them, and the son need not obey on this, while he keeps honouring her in other ways. — [binbaz.org.sa fatwa 7989](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/7989/حكم-طاعة-الام-اذا-امرت-بطلاق-الزوجة); [binbaz.org.sa fatwa 19200 "ليس من المعروف أن تطلق زوجتك بدون جرم ولو بأمر والدتك"](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/19200/%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%B3-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82%C2%A0%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%83-%D8%A8%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%85-%D9%88%D9%84%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AA%D9%83)
- **Ibn ʿUthaymīn (via islamqa):** he distinguishes two cases. If the parent gives a valid sharʿī reason, such as the wife's conduct being suspect, the son should comply. If the demand stems from jealousy or dislike, he need not divorce; he should keep the wife and win over the parent gently. The answer also quotes **Ibn Taymiyya**: it is not lawful to divorce because the mother says so, and divorcing is not part of birr. — [islamqa 133184](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/133184); [islamqa 47040](https://islamqa.info/ar/47040)
- islamqa also has related answers: a mother wanting her son to divorce his second wife ([islamqa 44923](https://islamqa.info/ar/44923)), and a wife's mother urging divorce ([islamqa 189687](https://islamqa.info/ar/answers/189687)).

- "نصائح لحيران بين أمه وزوجته" (fatwa 66452). — [Islamweb](https://islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/66452/%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD-%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A3%D9%85%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%87)

### Inferences
- A Bengali compilation could be organised as follows:
  1. The status of the mother (birr) and the status of the wife (ḥusn al-ʿishra).
  2. Do they conflict? Precedence rules, including nafaqa and the wife's right to separate housing.
  3. The mother ordering divorce (Ibn Bāz, Ibn ʿUthaymīn, Ibn Taymiyya).
  4. Is the wife obliged to serve the mother-in-law? (No, but it is iḥsān.)
  5. Practical advice to the husband, the mother and the wife (Islamweb, Alukah, islamway).

  This would serve the Bengali reader more fully than any single book located.
- The safest sources to compile from are the deceased major scholars (Ibn Bāz, Ibn ʿUthaymīn), quoted with attribution and with permission from their foundations for print. Living sources (islamqa, Islamweb, Alukah) need permission.
