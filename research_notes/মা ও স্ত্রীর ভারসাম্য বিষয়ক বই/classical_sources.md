# Classical (pre-1900) Arabic sources on balancing a mother's rights and a wife's rights

Method note: web search worked, but direct page fetches were blocked in this session for shamela.ws, islamqa.info, ar.wikisource.org and kalemtayeb.com. As a result, several volume/page references below come from secondary citations (search snippets, fatwa sites) and are marked **[UNVERIFIED]**. One primary text, Ibn al-Jawzi's *Birr al-Walidayn* (Shamela 0000244 via OpenITI on GitHub), was downloaded and checked directly.

## Q1. Is there a classical standalone treatise (risala) specifically on mother vs. wife, or on obeying parents in divorce?

### Takeaway
I found no pre-1900 standalone risala devoted specifically to "mother vs. wife" or "طاعة الوالدين في الطلاق". The classical material is either (a) short general treatises on *birr al-walidayn* that include the key narrations (Ibn al-Jawzi, al-Tartushi), or (b) self-contained fatwas and sections inside larger works (Ibn Taymiyya, Imam Ahmad via Ibn Muflih, al-Saffarini). A Bengali book on this theme would therefore most likely be either a translation of a short *birr* treatise with a thematic appendix, or a compiled anthology of the classical fatwas and sections.

### Cited Findings
- A search for a manuscript or risala titled on "obedience to parents in divorce" found nothing. Results were modern fatwas (Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya no. 14050 and no. 15448; Ibn Baz) and one unspecified "رسالة في الطلاق" manuscript on al-Alukah, whose content and author are not stated. — [Dar al-Ifta 14050](https://dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/14050/حكم-طاعة-الوالدين-في-الأمر-بالطلاق); [Dar al-Ifta 15448](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/Fatwa/Details/15448/طاعة-الوالدين-في-طلاق-الزوجة); [Alukah manuscript listing](https://www.alukah.net/homayed/11534/49974/%d9%85%d8%ae%d8%b7%d9%88%d8%b7%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d8%b3%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b7%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%82/)
- The core classical positions, as modern fatwas cite them:
  - Ibn Taymiyya held that a son whose mother orders him to divorce his wife "لا يحل له أن يطلقها، بل عليه أن يبرها، وليس تطليق امرأته من برها". — [search snippet of al-Saffarini quoting Ibn Taymiyya (kalemtayeb)](https://kalemtayeb.com/safahat/item/94979); also cited by [IslamOnline (Atiyya Saqr fatwa PDF)](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/wp-content/uploads/pdf/fiqh-islamonline-net-2534.pdf)
  - Imam Ahmad was asked about a father who orders his son to divorce. He said "لا تطلقها", and when the questioner cited 'Umar and Ibn 'Umar he replied "حتى يكون أبوك مثل عمر". This is reported via Ibn Muflih's *al-Adab al-Shar'iyya*. — [Alukah (al-Zuman, "متى تجب طاعة الوالدين؟")](https://www.alukah.net/refai/15/41478/%d9%85%d8%aa%d9%89-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a8-%d8%b7%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%86%d8%9f/); [Dar al-Ifta 18334](https://dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/18334/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%85%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A9)
  - al-Hasan al-Basri is quoted as saying divorce "ليس من البر في شيء". — [Alukah (al-Zuman)](https://www.alukah.net/web/zoman/0/41478/%d9%85%d8%aa%d9%89-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%a8-%d8%b7%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%86%d8%9f/)
  - Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyya (fatwa 18334) states that the jurists agree a son need not obey his parents in divorcing his wife absent a shar'i cause, and that refusing is not 'uquq. It cites al-Haytami's *al-Zawajir* for the definition of 'uquq and Ibn Muflih's *al-Adab al-Shar'iyya*. — [Dar al-Ifta 18334](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/18334/%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%85%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A9)
- Contrasting modern view (not classical, for context only): Ibn Baz advised in one case that it was better to divorce out of regard for the parents. — [binbaz.org.sa fatwa 5962](https://binbaz.org.sa/fatwas/5962/حكم-طاعة-الوالدين-في-طلاق-الزوجة)
- Atiyya Saqr (modern, Azhar) distinguishes a father's order to divorce, which may be followed if it rests on a valid religious reason, from a mother's. — [IslamOnline PDF](https://fiqh.islamonline.net/wp-content/uploads/pdf/fiqh-islamonline-net-2534.pdf)

### Inferences
- The "mother vs. wife" theme in classical literature turns on two narrations: Ibn 'Umar (his father 'Umar ordered him to divorce, and the Prophet said "أطع أباك") and Abu al-Darda' (a mother ordered divorce, and he replied "لا آمرك أن تطلقها ولا آمرك أن تعصي أمك… الوالدة أوسط أبواب الجنة"). The fiqh texts (Ahmad, Ibn Taymiyya, Ibn Muflih, al-Saffarini) all reconcile these narrations. A Bengali book could be built around them.

### Gaps
- I could not search manuscript catalogues (King Faisal Center, Dar al-Kutub, Jami' al-Shamela's manuscript index) for a dedicated risala. The absence of one is a negative finding from web search only.
- Hanafi, Maliki and Shafi'i primary texts on parents ordering divorce were not reached directly. One news site attributes to the Shafi'is the view that such an order without shar'i need is "حمق لا يلتفت إليه", but it gives no primary citation. — [Sada El-Balad](https://www.elbalad.news/5836964) **[UNVERIFIED primary source]**

## Q2. Bibliographic detail for each candidate text and the relevant sections

### Takeaway
The best candidate for a standalone translation is Ibn al-Jawzi's short *Birr al-Walidayn* (Shamela 244). It is in the public domain, short at 25 sections, and contains both key narrations. The strongest fiqh material on mother vs. wife is in Ibn Taymiyya's fatwa, Ibn Muflih's *al-Adab al-Shar'iyya*, and al-Saffarini's *Ghidha' al-Albab*. These are suitable as appended excerpts but not as whole books, since they are multi-volume works.

**6. غذاء الألباب في شرح منظومة الآداب (*Ghidha' al-Albab fi Sharh Manzumat al-Adab*), Muhammad b. Ahmad al-Saffarini al-Hanbali (d. 1188 AH)**
- It contains a discrete section titled «إذا أمر الأب أو الأم ولدهما بتطليق زوجته يجيبهما أم لا؟». The section:
  - quotes *al-Adab al-Kubra* that a father's order does not obligate divorce, which "most of the companions" (of the madhhab) hold;
  - gives Imam Ahmad's "لا تطلقها… حتى يكون أبوك مثل عمر";
  - quotes Ibn Taymiyya's fatwa on the mother;
  - argues from «لا ضرر ولا ضرار» that obeying either parent in a divorce motivated by mere caprice is not obligatory. — [kalemtayeb excerpt](https://kalemtayeb.com/safahat/item/94979) (content per search snippet; direct fetch blocked)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** The volume and page number and the editions are not confirmed. It is a multi-volume work. This section is probably the most compact and self-contained classical treatment of the exact question and is a good candidate for a translated appendix.

**9. الأدب المفرد (*al-Adab al-Mufrad*), al-Bukhari (d. 256 AH)**
- Opens with the parents chapters (*birr al-walidayn*). Bengali translations are widely available (see Q3). It is a hadith/athar collection rather than a fiqh discussion of the conflict. — [Rokomari listing](https://www.rokomari.com/book/222570)

### Inferences
- Among the texts found, only Ibn al-Jawzi's short *Birr al-Walidayn* is short, standalone, freely available online in full and public domain, and it already contains both pivotal narrations. It is the most practical base text, but it is a general *birr* work and is not centred on the wife.
- A Bengali book built specifically on "মা ও স্ত্রীর ভারসাম্য" would most likely work as a compiled anthology:
  1. Ibn al-Jawzi's *Birr al-Walidayn*, in full or in part;
  2. al-Saffarini's *Ghidha' al-Albab* section on parents ordering divorce;
  3. Ibn Taymiyya's fatwa;
  4. Imam Ahmad's statements from Ibn Muflih;
  5. al-Ghazali's sections on the rights of parents and the rights of wives.

### Gaps

## Q3. Which of these already have Bengali translations?

### Takeaway
No Bengali translation of Ibn al-Jawzi's *Birr al-Walidayn* or *al-Birr wa al-Sila*, al-Tartushi's *Birr al-Walidayn*, al-Saffarini's *Ghidha' al-Albab*, or Ibn Muflih's *al-Adab al-Shar'iyya* was found. *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* and al-Ghazali's *Ihya'* (including the marriage volume) do exist in Bengali. That leaves room for a new Bengali translation centred on Ibn al-Jawzi plus the fiqh texts.

### Cited Findings
- A Bengali search for Ibn al-Jawzi's parents book returned only an English translation, *At Their Feet*, and no Bengali edition. — [kalamullah.com](https://kalamullah.com/at-their-feet.html)
- *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* is available in Bengali on Rokomari. Listings include a 530-page edition (4th ed., 2017) and a 2-volume Arabic-Bengali edition (2023, 1,072 pp.). — [Rokomari 222570](https://www.rokomari.com/book/222570); [Rokomari 475517](https://www.rokomari.com/book/475517)
- "মা-বাবাকে ভালোবাসুন" (Ar-Rihab Publications), translated by Abdullah Rafiq, combines material attributed to Imam al-Bukhari with commentary by Sa'id b. 'Ali al-Qahtani, a modern author. It is a mixed, not purely classical, book. — Rokomari search results ([Rokomari 387986 images](https://www.rokomari.com/book/387986/95bf03e55_387986-32.jpg)) **[UNVERIFIED that 387986 is this exact title]**
- al-Ghazali's *Ihya'* exists in Bengali in volumes. Volume 5, "ইহয়াউ উলূমিদ্দীন - ৫ (বিয়েশাদি ও জীবিকা)", covers marriage. — [Rokomari 218487](https://www.rokomari.com/book/218487/ihyau-ulumiddeen-5)
- Modern Bengali books on spousal rights exist, e.g. "স্বামী-স্ত্রীর অধিকার" (80 pp., 2022) and Mufti Arif Mahmud's "স্ত্রীকে ভালোবাসুন". No classical mother-vs-wife text appeared among them. — [Rokomari 257555](https://www.rokomari.com/book/257555); [Rokomari 475520](https://www.rokomari.com/book/475520)

### Inferences
- A Bengali translation of Ibn al-Jawzi's *Birr al-Walidayn*, especially with an appendix of the Ahmad, Ibn Taymiyya and al-Saffarini texts on parents ordering divorce, appears not to duplicate any existing Bengali title found here.

## Q4. Public domain and copyright status

### Takeaway
All the authors died between 256 and 1188 AH, so the original Arabic texts are public domain. Modern critical apparatus may carry copyright: introductions, footnotes, takhrij and editorial corrections, e.g. by 'Abd al-Mawjud and Mu'awwad (1993) or Khalid al-Tartusi (2014). Translating the bare *matn* is safe. Copying a modern editor's footnotes or takhrij needs permission or independent re-sourcing.

### Cited Findings
- Death dates: Ibn al-Jawzi 597 AH, per the Shamela/OpenITI metadata — [OpenITI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenITI/0600AH/master/data/0597IbnJawzi/0597IbnJawzi.BirrWalidayn/0597IbnJawzi.BirrWalidayn.Shamela0000244-ara1). al-Tartushi 520 AH — [alkitab.com](https://alkitab.com/44240.html).
- Modern edited editions with named editors: *al-Birr wa al-Sila*, ed. 'Abd al-Mawjud & Mu'awwad (1993) — [catalogue](https://research.kwaretech.com/Record/in00000460236/UserComments?print=1). The al-Tartushi/Ibn al-Jawzi volume, ed. Khalid al-Tartusi (2014) — [alkitab.com](https://alkitab.com/44240.html).
- The Shamela 244 text lists no editor (NODATA), so its textual basis is undocumented. — [OpenITI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenITI/0600AH/master/data/0597IbnJawzi/0597IbnJawzi.BirrWalidayn/0597IbnJawzi.BirrWalidayn.Shamela0000244-ara1)

### Inferences
- For a publishable translation, use the Shamela text as a working copy and collate it against a printed edition: the 2014 Dar al-Kitab al-'Arabi volume, or the 1993 *al-Birr wa al-Sila* if that turns out to be the same work. Cite the edition, but do not reproduce the editor's apparatus.

### Gaps
- OpenITI corpus licence terms were not verified in this session.
- The copyright status of specific Arab-country editions was not checked. This is general practice, not legal advice.
