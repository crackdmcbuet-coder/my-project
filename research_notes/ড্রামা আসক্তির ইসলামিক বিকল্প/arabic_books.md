# Arabic Books (Classical and Modern) to Translate into Bangla as an Islamic Alternative to Romantic Dramas, Reels and Romantic Fantasy

> **Method and limits. Read this first.**
> - About 20 web searches were run (Bangla and Arabic queries). **rokomari.com could not be fetched in this session** (DNS blocked by the sandbox network policy). Bangla-translation status therefore rests on **search-engine snippets of rokomari/goodreads/archive.org pages**, not on full catalogue pages. wafilife.com was not reachable through search snippets at all.
> - Status codes used below:
>   - **BN-CONFIRMED**: a Bangla edition was seen in a search result (link given).
>   - **BN-PROBABLE**: a snippet points to a Bangla edition, but the publisher, translator or exact original was not confirmed.
>   - **BN-NOT FOUND**: targeted searches found no Bangla edition. This makes it a **candidate for translation**, but the user must still check rokomari/wafilife by hand before committing, because search coverage of Bangla e-commerce sites is poor.
> - Bibliographic details (death dates, editions, tahqiq, length) marked **[bg]** come from general bibliographic knowledge. They were **not re-verified on the web in this session** and should be checked against the Shamela/waqfeya record before use. Details without [bg] carry an inline source.
> - General places to find the Arabic text of nearly all the classical titles: [al-Maktaba al-Shamela](https://shamela.ws/) (searchable text), [waqfeya.net](https://waqfeya.net/) (PDF scans of printed tahqiq editions), [archive.org](https://archive.org/) (PDFs), [noor-book.com](https://www.noor-book.com/) (modern titles; check copyright).

## 1. Classical works on love, desire, gaze and 'ishq: what they are, and which already have Bangla translations

### Takeaway
Ibn al-Qayyim's **روضة المحبين ونزهة المشتاقين** is the single best-fit classical book (a full treatise on love, gaze, 'ishq and chastity), and **no Bangla translation was found**. It is the strongest translation candidate. Ibn al-Jawzi's **ذم الهوى** may already have a Bangla edition ("প্রবृত्তির নিন্দাচার"; unconfirmed). **الداء والدواء** could not be confirmed in Bangla despite being widely translated into English. A Bangla translation of Munajjid's short tract on following desire (اتباع الهوى) is confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **روضة المحبين ونزهة المشتاقين**, Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya. A Bangla biography lists it under this full Arabic title among his works ([pathagar.org biography](https://www.pathagar.org/article/detail/66), via search snippet). A Bangla news article on guarding the gaze quotes "রওজাতুল মুহিব্বিন, পৃষ্ঠা-১১৩": unrestrained looking unsettles the heart and breeds longing for what one cannot have ([zoombangla](https://inews.zoombangla.com/%E0%A6%A6%E0%A7%83%E0%A6%B7%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%BF-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%87-%E0%A6%95%E0%A7%8B%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%A8-%E0%A6%B9%E0%A6%BE/)). Two targeted Bangla searches ("রওজাতুল মুহিব্বীন … বাংলা অনুবাদ", "রাওদাতুল মুহিব্বীন / ভালোবাসার উদ্যান") found **no Bangla translation**; they returned only his other translated books (আর-রূহ, যাদুল মাআদ, সবর) ([rokomari Ar-Ruh](https://www.rokomari.com/book/182926/ar-ruho-rohosho-bishleshon-kobor-jogoter-bishod-biboron); [rokomari Mukhtasar Zad al-Ma'ad](https://www.rokomari.com/book/223657/mukhtasar-jadul-ma-ad)). → **BN-NOT FOUND, top candidate.**
- Ibn al-Qayyim's birth year is given as 691 AH ([islamhouse author page](https://islamhouse.com/en/author/60077), via search snippet). [bg] He died in 751 AH.
- **الداء والدواء / الجواب الكافي لمن سأل عن الدواء الشافي**, Ibn al-Qayyim. Two Bangla searches (under "আদ-দা ওয়াদ দাওয়া" and "আল-জাওয়াবুল কাফি") found **no confirmed Bangla translation**. One rokomari title, "গুনাহ থেকে ফিরে আসুন", mentions this book in its description, but the snippet does not show whether it is a translation ([rokomari 208639](https://www.rokomari.com/book/208639/gunah-theke-fire-ashun)). → **BN-UNCERTAIN.** The user should open that rokomari page to check whether it is a translation.
- **ذم الهوى**, Ibn al-Jawzi. A rokomari page (book 362582) refers to "ইবনুল জাওযি রহিমাহুল্লাহ-এর রচিত যাম্মুল হাওয়া বা প্রবৃত্তির নিন্দাচার". The rest of the snippet is about Ibn al-Qayyim, so it is unclear whether that page is a translation of ذم الهوى or only cites it ([rokomari 362582](https://www.rokomari.com/book/362582)). → **BN-PROBABLE, verify.**
- **عيون الحكايات**, Ibn al-Jawzi (a story collection of the righteous; a good "story" replacement). It has a **complete Bangla translation**, "সোনালি যুগের গল্পগুলো" (2 volumes in 1, first edition 2022, 960 pp.) ([rokomari 228402](https://www.rokomari.com/book/228402)). → **BN-CONFIRMED.**
- **صيد الخاطر**, Ibn al-Jawzi. A targeted Bangla search found no Bangla edition ([search returned only the Uyun al-Hikayat listing](https://www.rokomari.com/book/228402)). → **BN-NOT FOUND (verify).**
- **مفسدات القلوب: اتباع الهوى**, Muhammad Salih al-Munajjid (modern). A Bangla PDF titled "অন্তর বিধ্বংসী বিষয়ঃ প্রবৃত্তির অনুসরণ – মুহাম্মাদ সালেহ আল-মুনাজ্জিদ" is on archive.org ([archive.org](https://archive.org/download/20200728_20200728_1651/)). → **BN-CONFIRMED.** (Inference: other parts of the same مفسدات القلوب series, such as العشق and إطلاق البصر, may be translated too; not checked.)

### Inferences
- Of the classical books, the best "untranslated and high-fit" picks are **روضة المحبين** (full or abridged), **صيد الخاطر** (if verified untranslated), and **selected chapters of إغاثة اللهفان** on music and singing.
- Ibn al-Jawzi's ذم الهوى and al-Kharaiti's اعتلال القلوب need "selected and graded" (منتقى/تهذيب) translations rather than full ones, because of weak narrations and explicit poems.
- مصارع العشاق and, to a degree, طوق الحمامة work against the user's goal (they aestheticise romantic longing). Treat them as cautionary background, not as replacements.

### Gaps
- Whether "গুনাহ থেকে ফিরে আসুন" (rokomari 208639) is a translation of الداء والدواء could not be verified, because rokomari was unreachable.
- Bangla status of إغاثة اللهفان, طوق الحمامة, اعتلال القلوب, ذم الملاهي and العقوبات was not searched individually (tool budget).
- Edition and page details above are [bg] and should be checked on [Shamela](https://shamela.ws/) and [waqfeya](https://waqfeya.net/).

## 2. Modern Islamic literature that reads like a story or novel

### Takeaway
Ra'fat al-Basha's صور من حياة الصحابة/الصحابيات, several Adham Sharqawi titles, and some Ali al-Tantawi and Najib al-Kilani titles **already exist in Bangla**. Untranslated or unconfirmed candidates include Kilani's **عذراء جاكرتا**, **ليالي تركستان**, **عمر يظهر في القدس** and **الظل الأسود**, Bakathir's **وا إسلاماه**, Sharqawi's **حديث المساء**, **نبأ يقين** and **رسائل من النبي**, and Ayman al-Otoom's **يا صاحبي السجن**.

### Cited Findings
**Abd al-Rahman Ra'fat al-Basha**
- Bangla editions exist: "সাহাবীদের জীবনকথা" (1st ed. 2021, 468 pp.; out of stock), "সাহাবায়ে কেরামের ঈমানদীপ্ত জীবন" (2 vols), and "নারী সাহাবীদের ঈমানদীপ্ত জীবন" (= صور من حياة الصحابيات) ([rokomari 221639](https://www.rokomari.com/book/221639/sahabider-jibonkotha); related rokomari listings [100690](https://www.rokomari.com/book/100690), [79057](https://www.rokomari.com/book/79057), [172878](https://www.rokomari.com/book/172878)). → **BN-CONFIRMED** (صور من حياة الصحابة, الصحابيات). Whether **صور من حياة التابعين** is translated was not confirmed. [bg] It likely is, so check before choosing it.

**Adham Sharqawi (أدهم شرقاوي, Lebanese-Palestinian)**
- Arabic works, verified: **حديث الصباح**, **حديث المساء** (short stories, each followed by the lessons drawn from it), **نبأ يقين** (collected articles with wisdom and stories), **رسائل من القرآن**, **رسائل من النبي ﷺ** (300 "messages" from the Prophet on relationships, marriage, work and more), **مع النبي**, the novel **نبض**, and **عن شيء اسمه الحب** ([mawdoo3: أشهر كتب أدهم شرقاوي](https://mawdoo3.com/أشهر_كتب_أدهم_شرقاوي); [abjjad: رسائل من النبي](https://abjjad.com/book/2806480901/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%84-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A-%D8%B5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%85)).
- Already in Bangla: "উমরের সাথে যখন দেখা হলো" (= عندما التقيت عمر بن الخطاب), "নবীজির সাথে" (= مع النبي; 31 hadith stories, each followed by lessons), "কুরআনের চিঠি" (probably = رسائل من القرآن), "কুরআনের পাঠশালায়", "উসওয়াতুল হাসানাহ" ([rokomari 203038](https://www.rokomari.com/book/203038/nobijir-sathe-sm); [rokomari 399146](https://www.rokomari.com/book/399146/quraner-chithi); [rokomari 233560](https://www.rokomari.com/book/233560); [rokomari 462329](https://www.rokomari.com/book/462329)). → **BN-CONFIRMED** for these.
- No Bangla edition of **حديث المساء**, **حديث الصباح**, **نبأ يقين** or **رسائل من النبي** surfaced in the searches → **BN-NOT FOUND (verify)**. Note: Bangla publishers have been translating Sharqawi rapidly, so recheck rokomari.
- **Content caution [inference]:** عن شيء اسمه الحب and the novel نبض are literary-romantic in tone. They are not recommended as replacements without review.

**Najib al-Kilani (نجيب الكيلاني, Egypt, 1931–1995)**
- عذراء جاكرتا (*Virgin of Jakarta*) is set in Indonesia as communists prepare a coup; the heroine Fatima and her family represent Muslim society amid political turmoil. Kilani lived 1931–1995 ([jarirbooksusa listing](https://jarirbooksusa.com/16768.html); [Wikipedia: Najib Kilani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najib_Kilani); [UIN Sunan Kalijaga thesis record](https://digilib.uin-suka.ac.id/cgi/export/eprint/78666/BibTeX/digilib-eprint-78666.bib)).
- Bangla editions attributed to Kilani on rokomari: "আংগুরের রানী" (its introduction notes most of his writing is aimed at teens and youth), "স্বাধীনতার মিছিল", "ইসলামি ইতিহাসের গল্প: আল্লাহর সৈনিক" (a translation of *Rijalullah*), and an interview collection "বরেণ্য আরব সাহিত্যিকদের জবানে ইসলামি সাহিত্য" ([rokomari 372262](https://www.rokomari.com/book/372262); [rokomari 207507](https://www.rokomari.com/book/207507/sadhinotar-michil); [rokomari 101862](https://www.rokomari.com/book/101862); [rokomari 229404](https://www.rokomari.com/book/229404)). The Arabic originals of "আংগুরের রানী" (probably ملكة العنب [bg]) and "স্বাধীনতার মিছিল" were not confirmed.
- A Bangla search for "জাকার্তার কুমারী" found **no Bangla edition** of عذراء جاكرتا (same search). → **BN-NOT FOUND, good candidate.**
- [bg] Other Kilani Islamic-world novels: **ليالي تركستان** (Chinese Turkestan), **عمالقة الشمال** (Nigeria), **الظل الأسود** (Ethiopia), **عمر يظهر في القدس**, **قاتل حمزة**, **نور الله**, **رمضان حبيبي**. Bangla status not searched individually. **Caution:** some Kilani novels include romance subplots (chaste, but present).

**Ali al-Tantawi (علي الطنطاوي, Syria/Saudi, 1909–1999 [bg])**
- Bangla editions exist: "সমাজ জীবন: বোধ ও চৈতন্য" (his essays compiled by Mujahid Mamun Dirani), "হে আমার ছেলে" (= يا بني), "ইতিহাসের মহানায়কেরা" (probably = رجال من التاريخ), and a children's history-stories series on Goodreads ("দুই ভাইয়ের গল্প", tied to قصص من التاريخ). Tantawi's preface says he "dressed history in the garment of story without distorting it" ([rokomari 523664](https://www.rokomari.com/book/523664/somaj-o-jibon); [rokomari 148968](https://www.rokomari.com/book/148968/ha-amar-chela); [rokomari 214470](https://www.rokomari.com/book/214470/itihasher-mohanayokera); [Goodreads 43600318](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/43600318)). → **BN-CONFIRMED/PROBABLE** for رجال من التاريخ and قصص من التاريخ (at least partly).
- [bg] Still likely untranslated in full: **ذكريات علي الطنطاوي** (8 vols, memoir; excellent but very long), **صور وخواطر**, **مع الناس**, **يا ابنتي** (an epistle to young women about men's intentions and chastity, a very close fit for the user's theme; Bangla status not searched).

**Ali Ahmad Bakathir (علي أحمد باكثير)**
- A Bangla search for وا إسلاماه found **no Bangla edition** (no relevant results) ([search](https://www.rokomari.com/book/234566/zubdatul-bayan-fee-eedahil-quran) returned only unrelated tafsir). → **BN-NOT FOUND.**
- [bg] وا إسلاماه (1945) is a historical novel about Qutuz, the Mamluks and 'Ayn Jalut, long taught in Egyptian schools. **Caution:** it includes a love story (Qutuz–Jalnar), chaste and dignified, which makes it a model of "halal romance in a heroic frame". Other works: الثائر الأحمر, ملحمة عمر (a play). Avoid سلامة القس (a romance about a singer).

**Ayman al-Otoom (أيمن العتوم, Jordan, b. 1972)**
- Jordanian poet and novelist (PhD in Arabic literature). His novels include **يا صاحبي السجن** (2012), **يسمعون حسيسها** (2012, a prison-literature bestseller that is also controversial), **طعم الموت/ذائقة الموت** (2013) and **حديث الجنود** (2014, about the 1986 Yarmouk University protest). Academic work notes the Islamist orientation of his writing ([Ayman Otoom summary](https://cavac.at/cavacopedia/Ayman%20Otoom); [Thematic Study of al-ʿAtūm's Prison Novels, DOAJ](https://library.kab.ac.ug/Record/doaj-art-0ce7bfc69dcb4d889c1f293457a61091/Description)).
- No Bangla translation was found ([same search](https://library.kab.ac.ug/Record/doaj-art-0ce7bfc69dcb4d889c1f293457a61091/Description)). → **BN-NOT FOUND.** **Caution [inference]:** the prison novels contain graphic torture scenes and political content (Jordan/Syria), so they suit adults, not as a light romance replacement.

### Other modern names in the brief, from background knowledge only (not web-verified this session)
- **Abd al-Hamid Judah al-Sahhar**: the series **محمد رسول الله والذين معه** (about 20 short sira novels), plus **أميرة قرطبة** and others. [bg] Bangla status not searched.
- **Ahmad Khayri al-Umari** (Iraq): **شيفرة بلال** (a novel), **البوصلة القرآنية**, **ليطمئن عقلي**, **استرداد عمر**. [bg] Bangla status not searched; some of his books have Bangla editions according to general knowledge, so verify.
- **Imad al-Din Khalil**: historian and novelist of "Islamic literature" (الأدب الإسلامي). Specific novel titles were not verified this session, so they are listed here as a gap.
- **Muhammad Ahmad al-Rashid** (المنطلق, العوائق, الرقائق): da'wa and tarbiya prose, **not** fiction. Partial fit only.
- **Mahmud Shakir**: if the brief means Mahmud Shakir's multi-volume التاريخ الإسلامي, it is history, not narrative fiction. Low fit.
- **"Kareem al-Shathili"**: could not identify this author, so the name is not included. It may be a mistaken name.

### Inferences
- The best **untranslated story-format candidates** for youth and women who watch romantic dramas are: Kilani's **عذراء جاكرتا** and **ليالي تركستان** (heroism, faith, and a chaste heroine), Bakathir's **وا إسلاماه** (heroic history with a dignified love story), Tantawi's **يا ابنتي** (short and direct), Sharqawi's **حديث المساء** and **نبأ يقين** (reel-length stories), and Sharqawi's **رسائل من النبي** (includes marriage and relationship messages).
- Sharqawi and Tantawi read as **easy-medium** modern prose. Kilani and Bakathir are **medium** (literary MSA, some dialogue). Al-Otoom is **hard** (poetic, layered prose).

### Gaps
- Rights: modern authors (Sharqawi, al-Otoom, al-Umari, and the heirs of Kilani, Tantawi and Bakathir) are in copyright. Translation needs publisher permission. Not researched.

## 4. Wasting time, entertainment and screens

### Takeaway
Abu Ghuddah's **قيمة الزمن عند العلماء** is the classic modern Arabic text on the value of time. It has an English translation, but **no Bangla edition was found**, which makes it a strong, short, easy candidate. Its companion صفحات من صبر العلماء does appear in Bangla.

### Cited Findings
- Abu Ghuddah's book on time is published in English as *The Value of Time*: a collection of sayings, incidents and stories of early scholars showing how they valued time ([Al-Salam store](https://store.alsalam.ac.uk/products/the-value-of-time); [SeekersGuidance excerpt](https://seekersguidance.org/articles/knowledge/the-blessing-of-time-shaykh-abd-al-fattah-abu-ghuddah/)).
- A Bangla search did **not** find a Bangla edition of قيمة الزمن. It did find a Bangla Abu Ghuddah title, "জ্ঞান সাধনায় উলামায়ে কেরামের ত্যাগ ও কুরবানী" (probably = صفحات من صبر العلماء) ([Goodreads quotes page](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/95548822)). → قيمة الزمن: **BN-NOT FOUND (verify)**.

### From background knowledge, not web-verified this session
- **قيمة الزمن عند العلماء**, 'Abd al-Fattah Abu Ghuddah (d. 1417/1997), Maktab al-Matbu'at al-Islamiyya, Aleppo/Beirut; short (~150–200 pp. [bg]). Easy-medium. Suits all readers.
- **الوقت في حياة المسلم**, Yusuf al-Qaradawi; short. Note that the author is controversial for some Bangladeshi audiences.
- **Fatwas on watching series** (حكم مشاهدة المسلسلات): these exist on islamqa.info, islamweb.net (fatwa centre) and dorar.net as short fatwas, not books. Good for an appendix, not a translation project. Specific fatwa numbers were not retrieved this session.
- **Electronic addiction (الإدمان الإلكتروني)** from an Islamic perspective: no specific Arabic book title was verified this session.

### Gaps
- No verified Arabic book-length work on screen or electronic addiction from an Islamic author was found within the tool budget.
- Ibn Abi al-Dunya's ذم الملاهي (Section 1) is the closest classical analogue.
