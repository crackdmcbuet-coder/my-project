# Islamic Story/Emotion Content as a Replacement for Indian/Pakistani TV Dramas & Reels (for Bangladeshi Viewers): Channels, Podcasts, Series, Films, Apps

Research date: 2026-11-03. About 25 searches. Subscriber counts come from third-party trackers (vidIQ, SpeakRJ, Playboard, SocialCounts, Wikipedia infoboxes). They are approximate and often disagree. Anything I could not verify is listed under "Gaps". I have not invented any names. Note: islam.earid.net (a Bangla "top Islamic YouTube channels" list) was blocked in this session's network and could not be fetched.

## Q1. Bangla Islamic story/storytelling channels, podcasts and audio platforms

### Takeaway
Big, active, verified Bangla Islamic channels exist, but they are mostly lecture/waz-based (Shaikh Ahmadullah about 3.5M; Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan's lecture channel; Rain Drops Media's Bangla Seerah serial). I found no verified, large, *dedicated* Bangla Islamic story-drama/audio-drama channel. Small "Bangla Islamic Story / Islamic Golpo" channels exist, but their quality and sourcing are unvetted. For the "serial" feeling, Rain Drops Media's multi-part Bangla Seerah is the strongest verified Bangla fit.

### Cited Findings
- **Shaikh Ahmadullah (Bangla; lectures, Q&A)**: about 3.48M subscribers, 3,096 videos and 397.7M views (SpeakRJ, updated June 8, 2026). Other figures: vidIQ 3.53M, Wikipedia infobox 3.33M, and a vidIQ snapshot from May 2025 showing 3.14M. He is the founder and chairman of As-Sunnah Foundation, and the foundation's updates moved to a separate channel so this one carries only his lectures — [SpeakRJ](https://www.speakrj.com/audit/report/UCuxth2BimHUigZ344JhcFPw/youtube); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCuxth2BimHUigZ344JhcFPw); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaykh_Ahmadullah); [As-Sunnah Foundation – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/As-Sunnah_Foundation)
- **Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan (Bangla; youth-oriented lectures on tafsir, hadith and social issues)**: Wikipedia calls him a conservative Bangladeshi Islamic speaker who gained recognition "especially among the youth". His 2021 disappearance became a subject of national discussion. The channel description says its aims include "combating radical ideologies and extreme views among the youth". A "Abu Tawha Muhammad Adnan Lectures" channel shows about 290K subscribers, 9.4M views and 293 videos on Playboard (capture date unknown). Several similarly named channels exist (the main "Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan" channel, created Aug 13, 2017, plus TawHaaTv), so counts depend on which channel is meant — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Toha_Muhammad_Adnan); [Playboard](https://playboard.co/en/channel/UCmZr-aiuaGqHhf1broxhVZA); [YouTube Fandom](https://youtube.fandom.com/wiki/Abu_Taw_Haa_Muhammad_Adnan); [YouTube channel](https://m.youtube.com/c/AbuTawHaaMuhammadAdnan)
- **Rain Drops Media (Bangla; Seerah audio/lecture serial)**: handle @RaindropsmediaOrg2015, linked to raindropsmedia.org. Its best-known series is a multi-part Bangla "Seerah" (life of the Prophet ﷺ), with episodes such as "03. Bangla Seerah – Status of Pre-Islamic World", plus playlists on Ramadan and the afterlife. I could not confirm the current subscriber count or upload activity, and most search hits are years old — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@RaindropsmediaOrg2015); [Pinterest episode pin](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/861524603697807612/); [RaindropsTV playlists](https://m.youtube.com/c/RaindropsTV/playlists)
- **"Bangla Islamic Story" (YouTube /istory)**: it describes itself as building a collection of authentic Islamic knowledge in Bangla, mainly prophets' stories in video form. Size and sourcing are unverified — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/istory)
- Generic "Bangla Islamic Golpo" videos and live streams also exist, for example "নবীদের জীবনের ৪টি অলৌকিক ও শিক্ষণীয় ঘটনা" and a story titled "মরুর বুকে এক অদ্ভুত প্রতারণা আর ঈমানের পরীক্ষা! ইসলামিক গল্প" (looks fictionalised). Their authenticity is unvetted — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HV10VnCE3V4); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wQcI6K8HalU)
- **Muslim Parenting Bangla**: a YouTube channel of educational videos on raising children according to Islamic teachings — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCruCOu1IV3x5S9XVX4VamLg)
- An older Bangla directory names "Sohoj Islam", "The Believer Bangla" and "Bismillah Tv" as Bangla Islamic channels. The list is about 4 years old and I did not verify their current activity — [islam.earid.net (via search snippet only)](https://islam.earid.net/bangla-islamic-youtube/)
- **ShunBoi (Bangla audiobook app)**: Dhaka-based, founded 2021. It offers audiobooks, short stories, book summaries and podcasts. One Play Store reviewer quotes about 100 taka/month. Rating 3.9 from 98 reviews, with complaints about player bugs and a limited catalogue. I found **no confirmation** that it carries an Islamic section or Sean Publication titles — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shunboi&hl=en_GB&gl=US); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shunboi); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJH9o-xdNVqJINowuhO6k-g)

### Inferences
- The Bangla market is dominated by lecture/waz formats, not narrative/serial formats. A "drama replacement" for Bangla viewers will likely need to combine (a) a serial-style Bangla Seerah (Rain Drops), (b) story-heavy lectures from big speakers, and (c) English story serials (Q3) for those who understand English.
- The small "Islamic Golpo" channels often fictionalise and sometimes use background music or AI visuals. Recommend them only with a caution to check sources (weak or fabricated narrations are a known risk in this genre).

### Gaps
- Could not verify that these named channels/platforms exist or are active: "Sean Publication audio", "Mizan Media", "Ilm Gallery", "Tarbiyah" (Bangla), "Muslim Media Bangla", "Boitoi" Islamic section. Do not list them as verified without a manual check.
- No current subscriber count for Rain Drops Media.
- No verified dedicated Bangla Islamic audio-drama (radio-play style) series was found.

## Q2. Bangla channels on marriage, family, self-improvement, lowering the gaze, overcoming addiction (youth speakers), plus controversies

### Takeaway

Shaikh Ahmadullah's large channel covers family and fiqh Q&A. I found no reliable source on a dedicated Bangla channel for marriage, gaze or addiction.

### Cited Findings
- Abu Taw Haa Muhammad Adnan gained recognition "especially among the youth" for lectures on tafsir, hadith and contemporary social issues. His 2021 disappearance "became a subject of widespread national discussion" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Toha_Muhammad_Adnan)

- Muslim Parenting Bangla covers family and child-raising — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCruCOu1IV3x5S9XVX4VamLg)
- A Bangla news piece on Islamic podcasts for self-development lists family topics (marital relations, child rearing, kinship ties) but names no specific channels — [Zoom Bangla](https://inews.zoombangla.com/%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%A4%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%89%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%BC%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%87-%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95-%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%A1%E0%A6%95/)

## Q3. English story/serial content (Yaqeen, Omar Suleiman, Mufti Menk, NAK, One Message Foundation, Muslim Central, Belal Assaad, etc.)

### Takeaway
The single best verified "serial" replacement in English is Yaqeen Institute's **"The Firsts"** with Dr. Omar Suleiman: about 185 episodes, each telling one Companion's story, and still releasing in 2026 (Apr 2026 episodes). It is available on YouTube and as a podcast. Mufti Menk (about 6.3M) is huge for short emotional reminders. Nouman Ali Khan's 2017 misconduct allegations should be noted neutrally.

### Cited Findings
- **The Firsts (Yaqeen Institute, Dr. Omar Suleiman)**: the Yaqeen series page lists 185 episodes, each telling the story of one Companion. Recent entries include Thawban (ra) (Apr 13, 2026), Safina (ra) (Apr 7, 2026), Julaybib (ra) "The Most Beautiful Story" (Nov 2025), and Abdullah ibn Umar (ra) and Qutham ibn Abbas (ra) (Aug 2025). It is also a podcast on Buzzsprout, Pocket Casts and Castbox. Podcast aggregators show lower episode counts (134–170), probably out of date — [Yaqeen series page](https://yaqeeninstitute.org/series/the-firsts.md); [Buzzsprout](https://thefirsts.buzzsprout.com/1194665/episodes); [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com/podcasts/7e375a80-a4ee-0138-e678-0acc26574db2)
- **Yaqeen Institute YouTube**: about 2.2M–2.4M subscribers (Feedspot list / other source). **Omar Suleiman's personal channel**: about 535K per SocialCounts (Playboard's 162K looks outdated) — [Feedspot](https://videos.feedspot.com/islamic_youtube_channels/); [SocialCounts](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCtm8rtofLSnaIBi3noB0INg); [Playboard](https://playboard.co/en/search?q=Yaqeen)
- "Angels in Your Presence" exists as an Omar Suleiman book (Kube Publishing, ISBN 9781847741509). I did not separately verify the video/podcast series — [Abbey's](https://abbeys.com.au/book/angels-in-your-presence-9781847741509.do)

## Q5. Visual series and films (Omar, The Message, Turkish series, animation, Iranian Prophet Yusuf), with scholarly views

### Takeaway
Every popular "Islamic drama" carries a fiqh issue. **Omar (2012)** depicts sahaba: Qaradawi and Salman al-Awda approved the script, while Al-Azhar and the Saudi Grand Mufti opposed it. The Iranian **Prophet Joseph** depicts a prophet and was rejected by Al-Azhar and the MWL Fiqh Academy. **Ertuğrul** has music, unveiled women and free-mixing, and scholars' answers (e.g. SeekersGuidance) deem it impermissible to watch. **The Message (1976)** and **Muhammad: The Last Prophet (2004, animated)** never show the Prophet ﷺ, but The Message shows sahaba (Hamza by Anthony Quinn). Kids' animation (Omar & Hana) is the least contentious, though it uses nasheed/music in some versions.

### Cited Findings
- **Omar (MBC/Qatar TV, 2012)**: 31 episodes, aired Ramadan from July 20, 2012, reported cost US$50M. A scholar board vetted the script, and Yusuf al-Qaradawi and Salman al-Awda approved it. **Al-Azhar issued a fatwa against it** (portraying the Prophet and Companions is forbidden). Saudi Grand Mufti Abdul-Aziz Al ash-Sheikh condemned it. A Kuwaiti lawyer sued to stop it. Some Shia scholars objected to its portrayal of Ali. An Egyptian administrative court ruling banning it is reported (date unconfirmed) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_(TV_series)); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/scholars-split-on-ramadan-series-1.386991); [Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/47154.aspx); [Globe and Mail](https://theglobeandmail.com/news/world/arabic-tv-series-depicting-islamic-figure-triggers-backlash/article4437208/)
- **Prophet Joseph (Yousuf-e Payambar, Iran 2008, dir. Farajollah Salahshoor)**: described as the most-watched Iranian TV series. Al-Azhar's Islamic Research centre rejected its airing on Egyptian channels, citing its rulings against impersonating prophets. The rule dates to 1926 and was later extended to Companions and family. The Muslim World League's Fiqh Academy (Makkah) also rejected it. A Saudi ban was reported. Note: an Al-Azhar scholar acknowledged that no explicit Quranic verse bans it, but said Sunni scholars agreed on prohibition. Shia scholarship is generally more permissive — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prophet_Joseph_(TV_series)); [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/articles/2010%2F10%2F03%2F121082.html); [Egypt Independent](https://egyptindependent.com/iranian-series-about-prophet-joseph-reignites-visual-depiction-debate/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/series-about-prophet-joseph-gets-saudi-ban-1.740509)
- **The Message (1976, Moustapha Akkad)**: the Prophet ﷺ is never seen or heard, and the camera takes his point of view. Anthony Quinn plays Hamza (ra), so Companions *are* depicted. Al-Azhar's depiction rule reportedly applied to its script — [Muhammad in film – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_in_film); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/parallels/2016/08/07/485234999/40-years-on-a-controversial-film-on-islams-origins-is-now-a-classic); [Egypt Independent](https://egyptindependent.com/iranian-series-about-prophet-joseph-reignites-visual-depiction-debate/)
- **Muhammad: The Last Prophet (2004, animated)**: produced by Badr International "according to the same aniconic principles as The Message". US release Nov 2004 by Fine Media Group — [Muhammad in film – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_in_film)
- **Diriliş: Ertuğrul**: a SeekersGuidance answer (Mawlana Ilyas Patel) notes music, women characters and mixing, and concludes it "is not permissible for us to see". A Seekers Path page notes scholarly difference on music and the rule against looking at unveiled women. Defenders (inzaar.org) say it has no obscene scenes. Then-PM Imran Khan promoted the Urdu dub on PTV (2020) — [SeekersGuidance](https://seekersguidance.org/uncategorized/can-we-watch-turkish-islamic-series-like-ertugrul-ghazi/); [Seekers Path](https://www.seekerspath.co.uk/?p=10085); [Inzaar](https://inzaar.org/?p=6809); [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1593201); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/entertainment/hot-on-web/ertugrul-gazi-muslims-viral-show-why-does-it-work)
- **Bilal: A New Breed of Hero**: an animated UAE film (Barajoun Entertainment, Abu Dhabi) retelling the story of Bilal ibn Rabah (ra). Voice cast includes Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje, Jacob Latimore and China Anne McClain — [The Native](https://thenativemag.com/watch-trailer-bilal-new-breed-hero-animated-film-starring-adewale-akinnuoye-agbaje/); [List of Islam-related animated films – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Islam-related_animated_films)
- **Omar & Hana (Digital Durian, Malaysia, 2017)**: a kids' series teaching Islamic values. Reported figures range from 3.5M to over 4.4M subscribers and 1.5–1.8B YouTube views, and TRT cites over 3.2B views overall. The Arabic channel passed 100K in 2021. The makers run the **Durioo+** streaming app — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_%26_Hana); [TRT World](https://www.trtworld.com/article/12759807); [The Vibes](https://thevibes.com/articles/culture/18448/arabic-omar-hana-youtube-channel-surpasses-100000-subscribers); [Crescent Wealth](https://crescentwealth.com.au/news-centre/featured/crescent-wealth-has-partnered-with-omar-and-hanna-founders-to-launch-durioo-a-high-quality-islamic-cartoon-streaming-app)
- **Zaky & Friends (One4Kids)**: described as a well-known Islamic kids' animation. No subscriber data found — [Sahlah blog](https://sahlah.net/blog/best-16-islamic-animations-for-kids-a-guide-to-fun-and-educational-content)

### Inferences
- A hierarchy by fiqh caution, from least to most contested:
  1. Audio storytelling by scholars (The Firsts, Seerah serials).
  2. Kids' animation without prophets.
  3. Films that avoid depicting the Prophet but show sahaba (The Message, Bilal).
  4. Live-action sahaba dramas (Omar).
  5. Series with music, unveiled women and mixing (Ertuğrul / Kuruluş Osman).
  6. Depictions of prophets (Prophet Yusuf; rejected by mainstream Sunni bodies).
- Historical-accuracy caveat: Ertuğrul and Osman are largely fictionalised (Ertuğrul's life is thinly documented). This is from background knowledge and was not cited in this session.

### Gaps
- Kuruluş Osman, "Muhammad: The Final Legacy" (2008), Malaysian/Indonesian Islamic dramas, and Majid Majidi's "Muhammad: Messenger of God" (2015) were not verified in this session.
- No source confirmed Richard Rich as director of the 2004 animated film.

## Q6. Halal entertainment and streaming apps (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Verified and active: **Alchemiya** (UK, the "Muslim Netflix", app updated through Aug 2025), **Qalbox** (by Muslim Pro/Bitsmedia, launched 2022), **Durioo+** (Omar & Hana makers, kids), and **HilalPlay** (Turkish/Arabic shows vetted for family values). Smaller apps include Zolal, DeenTV, One Islam TV and Tajree. **"Ummah TV" and "HalalFlix" could not be verified.**

### Cited Findings
- **Alchemiya**: says it hand-picks and screens titles for "no nudity, swearing or any themes that go against Muslim values". Over 1000 hours of films, drama, documentaries and kids' content. Available on web, iOS/Android, Roku and Fire TV, with monthly/yearly subscriptions. Founder Navid Akhtar said about 10% of viewers were non-Muslim. The App Store shows updates through Aug 2025 — [alchemiya.com](https://alchemiya.com/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/alchemiya/id1005970501); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alchemiya); [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/economy/islamic-streaming-service-aims-to-celebrate-good-things/2182219); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alchemiya)
- **Qalbox (Muslim Pro / Bitsmedia)**: launched 2022 inside the Muslim Pro app and on the web. It offers documentaries, films, shows and Quran recitation, and was reported as gaining popularity in the Middle East — [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2022/08/02/worlds-largest-muslim-lifestyle-app-launches-a-ground-breaking-streaming-service-paving-way-for-a-new-era-of-muslim-entertainment-industry/); [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2456761/media)
- **Durioo+**: kids' halal shows "vetted by Muslim parents", with a Quran memorisation feature — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/id1605538612)
- **HilalPlay**: Turkish, Arabic and international shows "selected to meet family values" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1546488743)
- Others: Zolal (Arab family-values VOD), DeenTV, One Islam TV and Tajree (a scholar/reciter creator platform) — [Zolal](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/zolal/id1619538690); [DeenTV](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1079244732); [One Islam TV](https://mwm.ai/es/apps/one-islam-tv/1601390544); [Tajree](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/tajree/id6761548203)

### Inferences
- "Halal-screened" means no nudity or swearing. It does **not** necessarily mean no music, no unveiled actresses, or no depiction of sahaba. Users following stricter fatwas should know this.
- None of these apps has notable Bangla-language content, as far as found.

### Gaps
- "Ummah TV" and "HalalFlix" were not found. Alchemiya's current 2026 subscriber numbers are not available. Bangla content on these platforms is unverified.
