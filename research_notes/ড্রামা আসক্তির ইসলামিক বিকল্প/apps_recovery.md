## 2. Screen-time and reel-blocking tools (incl. Islamic-focused)

### Takeaway
The free tools with the best evidence or clearest value are: one sec (the only one with peer-reviewed data: 36% of attempted app openings were abandoned), ScreenZen (free, no paywall on iOS and Android), Unhook (free browser extension that hides YouTube Shorts and recommendations, but it does not work in the native app), plus the built-in Digital Wellbeing (Android) and Screen Time (iOS). Opal is iOS-first and mostly paid. "Haram Blocker" as an Android app was not found. Real Islamic-focused blockers are HaramBlock (Firefox add-on, also on Firefox Android), Deen Shield (Firefox, very few users) and HaramBlur (iOS, $9.99).

### Cited Findings
- one sec (Riedel et al., PNAS 2023): adds a breathing pause and deliberation message, plus an option to dismiss, before a chosen app opens. Across a 6-week field study of 280 existing users, 36% of attempts to open a target app ended with the user closing it. Limits: opt-in, no control group, a co-author is the developer, and it measured openings rather than total time. — [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2213114120); [critical review, getfinit](https://getfinit.com/blog/one-sec-review); [one sec research page](https://one-sec.app/research/)
- one sec is on Google Play (wtf.riedel.onesec) and has a browser extension. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=wtf.riedel.onesec&hl=en_US)
- ScreenZen: free on iOS and Android with optional tips, no subscription or paywall. Android needs accessibility permissions. One comparison table's "$4.99/month" claim conflicts with the others and looks like an outlier. — [WhistleOut review](https://www.whistleout.com/CellPhones/Apps/screenzen-app-review); [habitdoom comparison](https://habitdoom.com/blog/opal-vs-screenzen-jomo-one-sec)
- Opal: "free with in-app purchases" on the App Store. Third-party reviews put Pro near $99.99/yr, while one review says $19.99/yr, so the price is unclear and varies by region. iOS-first; the Android app came later and has fewer features. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/id1497465230); [makerstack review](https://makerstack.co/reviews/opal-review/); [cursedscreen](https://cursedscreen-website.pages.dev/blog/opal-alternative/)
- Unhook: free extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Hides YouTube related videos, Shorts, comments, homepage recommendations and trending. It works only in browsers, not the native YouTube app. — [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/unhook-remove-youtube-rec/khncfooichmfjbepaaaebmommgaepoid/); [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/hebpjnnclppdnfghdnmhgdljmjpfhggk)
- iOS Shorts blocker "Detoxify – Short Block YT" exists on the App Store. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6740556197)
- HaramBlock (Rasul Abu Muhammad Amin): free, open-source Firefox add-on, also on Firefox for Android. Uses on-device ML to mask parts of images and video for lowering the gaze. The developer admits some false positives and negatives. — [Mozilla Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/haramblock/)
- Deen Shield (Alhaq Studio): Firefox add-on that claims to block 10,000+ inappropriate sites, with optional social-media blocking and a new tab showing Quran and prayer times. Very small user base. — [Mozilla Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/fr/firefox/addon/deenshield/)
- HaramBlur: iOS-only on-device AI blur, $9.99. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6741595731)

### Inferences
- In Bangladesh, most youth are on budget Android phones. Free, offline-capable options therefore matter most: Digital Wellbeing (built-in timers and Focus mode), ScreenZen, one sec's free tier, and Unhook or HaramBlock in a mobile browser. A useful tactic is to uninstall the native YouTube/Facebook apps and use the browser with Unhook, which removes Shorts and Reels entry points.
- Paid tools such as Opal and HaramBlur are a poor fit for most Bangladeshi users because international card payment is needed.

### Gaps
- Not searched or verified: BlockSite, Cold Turkey, "Purify", "Ummah Pro", and Islamic habit trackers. No claims made.
- No Bangladesh-specific study of blocker effectiveness was found.
- The built-in Digital Wellbeing and Screen Time features were not sourced this session (they are standard OS features).

## 3. Psychology: why dramas, reels and romantic fantasy hook people, and evidence-based replacement

### Takeaway
Binge-watching addiction is a measurable construct linked to loneliness and emotion regulation. Short-video addiction research is early but shows attention and decision-making correlates. Romance-genre media in particular predicts unrealistic relationship expectations and lower commitment. On the replacement side, the strongest evidence is for implementation intentions ("if-then" plans; d = 0.65 in a 94-test meta-analysis, refined to d = 0.27–0.66 across 642 tests in 2024) and for friction-based environment design (one sec).

### Cited Findings
- The Binge-Watching Addiction Questionnaire was developed and validated to capture addiction-like features of series watching. — [PMC7926824](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7926824/)
- A 2025 study (PLOS ONE per secondary coverage) found loneliness was positively related to binge-watching **addiction**, framed as emotion-regulation coping. In a 551-adult sample, 334 (61%) met its addiction criteria. It covered TV series only, not TikTok or YouTube. — [PMC12822925](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12822925/); [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/binge-watching-loneliness-mental-health-impacts)
- Commentary on that study argues that emotional investment in parasocial relationships with fictional characters leaves less energy for real reciprocal relationships. This is an interpretation, not a finding. — [Asia Daily](https://asiadaily.org/news/12103/)
- Short-video addiction: a review of the Chinese context discusses causes, psychological mechanisms and coping strategies, and notes there are no established clinical diagnostic criteria yet. — [PMC11333346](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11333346/)
- An EEG study found that short-video use negatively affects attention functions. — [PMC11236742](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11236742/)
- People with short-video addiction symptoms showed altered brain responses during decision-making and lower loss aversion (small sample, n = 36). — [PsyPost](https://www.psypost.org/people-with-short-video-addiction-show-altered-brain-responses-during-decision-making/)
- Adolescent addiction to short-video apps is reviewed in a PMC article. — [PMC9127662](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9127662/)
- Romance media: preferring romantic comedies and dramas was linked to stronger "love conquers all" beliefs and higher intimacy expectations (Galloway et al., 2015). — [UKY Scholars](https://scholars.uky.edu/en/publications/does-movie-viewing-cultivate-young-peoples-unrealistic-expectatio/)
- Penn State Media Effects Lab: genre (romantic comedies, magazine love quizzes) predicted unrealistic expectations better than amount of viewing. — [Penn State](https://www.bellisario.psu.edu/medialab/research-article/the-effect-of-media-consumption-on-perceptions-of-romantic-relationships)
- Among 392 married people, stronger belief in TV romance was associated with lower commitment and more attraction to alternatives. — [LiveScience](https://www.livescience.com/23290-belief-in-tv-romances-could-hurt-your-love-life.html)
- An Indian study found that internalising screen-romance messages, rather than mere exposure, shaped beliefs. — [IJIP](https://ijip.in/wp-content/uploads/ArticlesPDF/article_619cfcf56f67841580ccab635d9960c7.pdf)
- Implementation intentions: Gollwitzer and Sheeran (2006), 94 tests and more than 8,000 participants, d = .65 for goal attainment; d = .77 for preventing derailment (relevant to resisting a cue such as "open YouTube"). — [KOPS](https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f); [Frontiers review](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2015.00395/full); [NCI archive](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/eot2008/20090813064657/http:/dccps.nci.nih.gov/brp/constructs/implementation_intentions/ii4.html)
- A 2024 Sheeran, Listrom and Gollwitzer meta-analysis of 642 tests found d = 0.27–0.66. Effects were larger with explicit if-then wording, high motivation, and rehearsed plans. — [siliconcanals summary](https://siliconcanals.com/?p=107751) (secondary source; primary not fetched)
- Friction or pause-based self-nudges reduce app openings (one sec, 36% abandoned). — [PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2213114120)

### Inferences (practical replacement plan built on the evidence above)
1. **Name the need.** Binge-watching tracks loneliness and emotion regulation, so the replacement must also meet the emotional need. That means connection (a halaqa or sisters' circle, family) and meaningful narrative (seerah, sahabiyat and Quranic stories), not just blocking.
2. **If-then plans.** Write 3–5 explicit plans and rehearse them, for example: "If I feel the urge to open Facebook Reels after Isha, then I will open the Al Hadith app and read 3 hadith." "If I'm lying in bed with my phone, then I put it outside the room and play a Quran or seerah audio."
3. **Friction.** Uninstall native YouTube/Facebook; use the browser with Unhook. Put one sec or ScreenZen on any app that remains. Turn on Digital Wellbeing grayscale (Bedtime mode) at night.
4. **Substitute the same "slot".** Replace story with story: seerah, the Quranic story of Yusuf, stories of the sahabiyat in Bangla audio or e-book (Boitoi). Replace short bursts with short bursts: one ayah with Bangla tafsir, or one hadith per urge.
5. **Romance and fantasy specifically.** The genre drives unrealistic expectations, so target romance genres first rather than all media. Pair this with the Islamic channelling of love (section 4: marriage, halal love, prophetic marital stories).
6. **Track.** Use the OS screen-time dashboards weekly. A relapse means revising the plan, not giving up.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study on limerence or romantic daydreaming specifically was retrieved, and no direct study on the "variable reward" mechanism in reels. The dopamine and variable-reward framing in popular sources is narrative, not direct evidence. Avoid strong neuroscience claims.
- No Bangladesh-specific prevalence data on drama or reel addiction was found.
- Lally et al. (2010) habit-formation timing (median ~66 days) was not verified this session.

## 4. Islamic framing: laghw, gaze, lahw al-hadith, thoughts (khawatir), fatwas, and channelling love

### Takeaway
The Quran praises believers who turn away from laghw (23:3), commands lowering the gaze for men and women (24:30–31), and condemns "lahw al-hadith" that leads astray (31:6). The hadith "من حسن إسلام المرء تركه ما لا يعنيه" sets the "leave what doesn't concern you" principle. Ibn al-Qayyim teaches stopping a thought early, before it becomes desire, then resolve, then action. Mainstream fatwas (IslamQA.info) rule most commercial dramas and soap operas impermissible because of immodesty, music and time-wasting. Islam channels the desire for love toward marriage, describing it as the best remedy for those who love each other, and gives prophetic models of marital affection.

### Cited Findings
**Quran** (standard references; quran.com URLs not fetched this session)
- 23:1–3: successful believers are "those who turn away from ill speech/vain talk (al-laghw mu'ridun)". — [Quran.com 23:3](https://quran.com/23/3)
- 24:30–31: believing men, then believing women, are told to lower their gaze and guard their chastity. — [Quran.com 24:30-31](https://quran.com/24/30-31)
- 31:6: "among the people is he who buys lahw al-hadith (idle tales/amusement) to mislead from the path of Allah without knowledge". Ibn Mas'ud's well-known tafsir applies it to singing. [Tafsir attribution not fetched; UNVERIFIED] — [Quran.com 31:6](https://quran.com/31/6)
- 30:21: Allah created spouses so that you find tranquility in them, and placed between you mawaddah and rahmah (affection and mercy). This is the Quranic frame for halal love. — [Quran.com 30:21](https://quran.com/30/21)
- 17:32: "Do not approach zina" covers the means, such as gaze and fantasy, not only the act. — [Quran.com 17:32](https://quran.com/17/32)

**Hadith** (standard collection numbering; sunnah.com pages not fetched this session, so verify numbering)
- "من حسن إسلام المرء تركه ما لا يعنيه" (part of a person's good Islam is leaving what does not concern him): Tirmidhi 2317; Nawawi's 40 Hadith #12. — [Sunnah.com Tirmidhi 2317](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2317); [Sunnah.com Nawawi 40 #12](https://sunnah.com/nawawi40:12)
- "Two blessings many people are deceived in: health and free time": Bukhari 6412. — [Sunnah.com Bukhari 6412](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6412)
- "O young people, whoever among you can afford marriage, let him marry, for it lowers the gaze and guards chastity…": Bukhari 5066. — [Sunnah.com Bukhari 5066](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5066)
- "We have not seen anything like marriage for two who love one another": Ibn Majah 1847. — [Sunnah.com Ibn Majah 1847](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:1847)
- Gaze: "Do not follow a glance with another glance; the first is yours, the second is not" (to Ali): Abu Dawud 2149. — [Sunnah.com Abu Dawud 2149](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:2149)
- Prophetic marital love: he named Aisha as the person most beloved to him (Bukhari 3662), and said of Khadijah, "I was granted her love" (Muslim 2435). **Ibn al-Qayyim on thoughts (khawatir)**
- *Al-Jawab al-Kafi / Ad-Da' wa ad-Dawa'* (1/157): passing thoughts do no harm unless pursued and engaged; a thought is like a passerby on the road, and if you ignore it, it passes. — [MuslimMatters](https://muslimmatters.org/2025/03/07/cast-aside-evil-thoughts-in-the-month-of-the-quran); [Wright Street Mosque](https://www.wrightstreetmosque.com/?p=2906)
- Attributed to *Al-Fawaid*: "Repel the thought; if you do not, it becomes a desire…" (the chain runs thought, then desire, then resolve/intention, then action, then habit). Neglecting a weak thought is like leaving a spark on dry firewood. This attribution rests on secondary sources; check the Arabic edition before quoting as *Al-Fawaid*. — [Goodreads quote collection](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/49338806); [Wright Street Mosque](https://www.wrightstreetmosque.com/?p=2906)
- An academic paper, "Regulations of Thinking in Islam through the Works of Ibn al-Qayyim", exists for a scholarly citation. — [Lark journal](https://lark.uowasit.edu.iq/index.php/lark/article/view/2672)

**Fatwas on dramas and series**
- IslamQA.info Q125535: watching movies and soap operas on public and private channels, and listening to music, is haram. — [IslamQA 125535](https://islamqa.info/en/125535)
- IslamQA.info Q171887: historical and Syrian dramas are haram because they distort history, waste time and display women's immodesty. — [IslamQA 171887](https://islamqa.info/en/171887)
- IslamQA.org (Hanafi, AskImam) Q127390 on Dirilis Ertugrul: "no nudity" alone does not make it permissible; it raises concerns about music, free mixing and wasted time. — [AskImam via IslamQA.org](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/127390/is-it-permissible-to-watch-dirilis-ertugrul/)
- IslamQA.info Q149104 treats watching video clips and soap operas as haram and discusses sitting with those who watch. — [IslamQA 149104](https://m.islamqa.info/en/answers/149104)
- A Bangla-language fatwa (IslamLand) says watching films, serials and song programmes containing obscenity or sin is forbidden. — [IslamLand (Bangla)](https://www.islamland.com/ben/fatawas/4006)
- A Shafi'i answer on TV in general (Shaykh Amjad Rasheed) also exists; its conclusion was not visible in the search. — [IslamQA.org Shafi'i 33793](https://islamqa.org/shafii/qibla-shafii/33793/what-is-the-ruling-on-watching-television/)

### Inferences
- The framing that persuades best for youth is probably not "haram list" first, but **time as amanah** (Bukhari 6412), **leaving what doesn't concern you** (Tirmidhi 2317), and **the gaze-and-thought chain** (24:30–31 plus Ibn al-Qayyim). The fatwas then follow as boundaries.
- Romantic fantasy and limerence map onto Ibn al-Qayyim's chain: the drama supplies the khatir, rumination grows it into desire. So the intervention is upstream (cut the input) plus redirection (dhikr, Quran audio, a real task).
- Islam does not suppress the desire for love. It validates it and channels it: marriage as the remedy (Ibn Majah 1847, Bukhari 5066), mawaddah and rahmah (30:21), and the Prophet's openly expressed love for Khadijah and Aisha. A plan for unmarried youth and women should include family conversations about timely, affordable marriage, fasting as the prophetic alternative (Bukhari 5066), and stories of marital love from the seerah as "halal romance narratives".

- Quran and hadith pages and the hadith numbering were not fetched this session. They are standard references, but the writer should spot-check them on quran.com and sunnah.com.
- Bangla Deobandi fatwa sites (e.g., ahlehaqmedia.com, Darul Uloom Deoband Bangla, Markazul Fikril Islami) were not retrieved, so I make no claims about their specific rulings.
- Not all scholars hold a blanket prohibition. Some permit clean, music-free, educational content. That more lenient view was not sourced this session.
- No verified Bangla Facebook groups or halaqa networks specifically targeting drama addiction were found.
