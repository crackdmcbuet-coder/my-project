## 1. Qur'anic verses on dreams (Yusuf 12:4-6, 12:36-49, 12:100-101; al-Saffat 37:102-105; al-Fath 48:27; al-Isra 17:60; Yunus 10:64)

### Takeaway
In the Qur'an, dreams of prophets are revelation (Ibrahim acts on his dream as a command). Dreams can be true and later fulfilled (Yusuf; the Prophet's dream of entering Makkah). The Qur'an also names a category of "confused dreams" (adghath ahlam, 12:44). Yunus 10:64 ("lahum al-bushra fil-hayat al-dunya") is explained in a hadith as the righteous dream a Muslim sees or is shown.

### Cited Findings

**Surah Yusuf (12):**
- **12:4**: إِذْ قَالَ يُوسُفُ لِأَبِيهِ يَا أَبَتِ إِنِّي رَأَيْتُ أَحَدَ عَشَرَ كَوْكَبًا وَالشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ رَأَيْتُهُمْ لِي سَاجِدِينَ
  বাংলা: "যখন ইউসুফ তার পিতাকে বলল, 'হে আমার পিতা! আমি (স্বপ্নে) এগারোটি নক্ষত্র এবং সূর্য ও চন্দ্রকে দেখেছি; দেখেছি, তারা আমাকে সিজদা করছে।'" — [Quran 12:4](https://quran.com/12/4) [K]
- **12:5** (do not tell the dream to those who may envy you; the basis for the hadith rule of telling dreams only to loved ones): قَالَ يَا بُنَيَّ لَا تَقْصُصْ رُؤْيَاكَ عَلَىٰ إِخْوَتِكَ فَيَكِيدُوا لَكَ كَيْدًا ۖ إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ لِلْإِنسَانِ عَدُوٌّ مُّبِينٌ
  বাংলা: "সে বলল, 'হে আমার বৎস! তোমার স্বপ্নের কথা তোমার ভাইদের কাছে বর্ণনা করো না; তাহলে তারা তোমার বিরুদ্ধে ষড়যন্ত্র করবে। নিশ্চয় শয়তান মানুষের প্রকাশ্য শত্রু।'" — [Quran 12:5](https://quran.com/12/5) [K]
- **12:6**: Allah will choose Yusuf and teach him "ta'wil al-ahadith" (interpretation of events/dreams): وَكَذَٰلِكَ يَجْتَبِيكَ رَبُّكَ وَيُعَلِّمُكَ مِن تَأْوِيلِ الْأَحَادِيثِ — [Quran 12:6](https://quran.com/12/6) [K]
- **12:36-41**: The two prisoners' dreams (one pressing wine, one carrying bread eaten by birds). Yusuf calls them to tawhid before interpreting, and the interpretation comes true ("قُضِيَ الْأَمْرُ الَّذِي فِيهِ تَسْتَفْتِيَانِ", 12:41). — [Quran 12:36-41](https://quran.com/12/36-41) [K]
- **12:43-49**: The King's dream (seven fat cows eaten by seven lean, seven green ears and seven dry). His court calls it **أَضْغَاثُ أَحْلَامٍ** ("jumbled/confused dreams", 12:44), but Yusuf interprets it as seven years of plenty followed by seven of famine. This shows that a non-believer's dream can also be true, and that a dream should be interpreted by someone with knowledge. — [Quran 12:43-49](https://quran.com/12/43-49) [K]
- **12:100**: هَٰذَا تَأْوِيلُ رُؤْيَايَ مِن قَبْلُ قَدْ جَعَلَهَا رَبِّي حَقًّا
  বাংলা: "এটাই আমার পূর্বেকার স্বপ্নের ব্যাখ্যা; আমার রব তা সত্যে পরিণত করেছেন।" The fulfilment of 12:4 came decades later. — [Quran 12:100](https://quran.com/12/100) [K]
- **12:101**: رَبِّ قَدْ آتَيْتَنِي مِنَ الْمُلْكِ وَعَلَّمْتَنِي مِن تَأْوِيلِ الْأَحَادِيثِ: dream interpretation is described as knowledge granted by Allah. — [Quran 12:101](https://quran.com/12/101) [K]

**Al-Saffat 37:102-105 (Ibrahim's dream):**
- **37:102**: يَا بُنَيَّ إِنِّي أَرَىٰ فِي الْمَنَامِ أَنِّي أَذْبَحُكَ فَانظُرْ مَاذَا تَرَىٰ ۚ قَالَ يَا أَبَتِ افْعَلْ مَا تُؤْمَرُ
  বাংলা: "হে আমার বৎস! আমি স্বপ্নে দেখেছি যে আমি তোমাকে যবেহ করছি; এখন বলো, তোমার অভিমত কী? সে বলল, 'হে আমার পিতা! আপনাকে যা আদেশ করা হয়েছে তা-ই করুন।'" The son's reply ("do what you are *commanded*") shows that a prophet's dream counts as revelation. — [Quran 37:102](https://quran.com/37/102) [K]
- **37:104-105**: وَنَادَيْنَاهُ أَن يَا إِبْرَاهِيمُ قَدْ صَدَّقْتَ الرُّؤْيَا ۚ إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ: "you have fulfilled the dream". — [Quran 37:104-105](https://quran.com/37/104-105) [K]

**Al-Fath 48:27 (the Prophet's dream before Hudaybiyya):**
- لَّقَدْ صَدَقَ اللَّهُ رَسُولَهُ الرُّؤْيَا بِالْحَقِّ ۖ لَتَدْخُلُنَّ الْمَسْجِدَ الْحَرَامَ إِن شَاءَ اللَّهُ آمِنِينَ مُحَلِّقِينَ رُءُوسَكُمْ وَمُقَصِّرِينَ لَا تَخَافُونَ
  বাংলা: "অবশ্যই আল্লাহ তাঁর রাসূলের স্বপ্নকে সত্যে পরিণত করেছেন; আল্লাহ চাইলে তোমরা অবশ্যই নিরাপদে মসজিদুল হারামে প্রবেশ করবে, কেউ মাথা মুণ্ডন করে, কেউ চুল ছেঁটে, নির্ভয়ে।" The dream was fulfilled in the 'Umrat al-Qada' (7 AH). — [Quran 48:27](https://quran.com/48/27) [K]

**Al-Isra 17:60 (caveat: probably not a sleep-dream):**
- وَمَا جَعَلْنَا الرُّؤْيَا الَّتِي أَرَيْنَاكَ إِلَّا فِتْنَةً لِّلنَّاسِ. Ibn 'Abbas explained this "ru'ya" as **رؤيا عين** (a waking vision with the eyes) shown to the Prophet on the night of Isra', not a sleep-dream (Sahih al-Bukhari 4716; also 3888). — [Quran 17:60](https://quran.com/17/60); [Bukhari 4716](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4716) [K]. *Report writer: present 17:60 carefully. It uses the word "ru'ya", but per Ibn 'Abbas it is not evidence about sleep-dreams.*

**Al-Anfal 8:43 (an additional dream verse):**
- إِذْ يُرِيكَهُمُ اللَّهُ فِي مَنَامِكَ قَلِيلًا: Allah showed the Prophet the enemy at Badr as few in his dream, to strengthen the believers. — [Quran 8:43](https://quran.com/8/43) [K]

**Al-Zumar 39:42 (sleep as "minor death", the basis of the bedtime dua):**
- اللَّهُ يَتَوَفَّى الْأَنفُسَ حِينَ مَوْتِهَا وَالَّتِي لَمْ تَمُتْ فِي مَنَامِهَا — [Quran 39:42](https://quran.com/39/42) [K]

**Yunus 10:62-64 ("lahum al-bushra") and its hadith tafsir:**
- أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ ۝ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَكَانُوا يَتَّقُونَ ۝ لَهُمُ الْبُشْرَىٰ فِي الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ
  বাংলা: "জেনে রাখো! আল্লাহর ওলিদের কোনো ভয় নেই, তারা চিন্তিতও হবে না: যারা ঈমান এনেছে এবং তাকওয়া অবলম্বন করত। তাদের জন্য সুসংবাদ দুনিয়ার জীবনে এবং আখিরাতে।" — [Quran 10:62-64](https://quran.com/10/62-64) [K]
- **Jami' al-Tirmidhi 2275** (Kitab al-Ru'ya, chapter on "lahum al-bushra fil-hayat al-dunya"): 'Ubada ibn al-Samit asked the Prophet ﷺ about this verse, and he said it is **الرؤيا الصالحة يراها المسلم أو تُرى له** ("the righteous dream that a Muslim sees or that is seen for him"). Also at Tirmidhi 3106 (tafsir), Ibn Majah 3898, Ahmad (~22687/22688). **Grading: al-Albani: sahih (al-Silsila al-Sahiha 1786).** Zubair 'Ali Za'i: chain weak/munqati' ('an'ana of Yahya ibn Abi Kathir). Others note a break between Abu Salama and 'Ubada but still accept the hadith through its supporting routes. — [tohed: Tirmidhi 2275](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/tirmidhi/2275); [islamtics: Tirmidhi 2275](https://islamtics.com/hadith/tirmidhi/2275/); [Tirmidhi 3106](https://islamtics.com/hadith/tirmidhi/3106/); [Ibn Majah 3898](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/ibn-majah/3898) [V]
- A similar narration from Abu al-Darda' is at Tirmidhi 2273. One secondary site attaches the same Albani grading (Sahiha 1786) to 2273, so **site numbering is inconsistent here**. — [islamtics: Tirmidhi 2273](https://islamtics.com/hadith/tirmidhi/2273/) [V for the inconsistency]
- A complementary sahih tafsir of "bushra" (people's praise of a righteous person as "عاجل بشرى المؤمن") is in Sahih Muslim 2642 (Abu Dharr). — [Muslim 2642](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2642) [K]

### Inferences
- The Qur'an gives three anchors used in the hadith: (a) true dreams exist and are fulfilled (12:100, 48:27); (b) confused dreams exist (أضغاث أحلام, 12:44), matching the hadith categories of *hulm*/self-talk; (c) do not share a dream with those who may envy (12:5), matching the hadith rule "tell only one you love".
- The tafsir of 10:64 as true dreams is acceptable to cite with a "sahih per al-Albani, with a disputed chain" caveat.

### Gaps
- Could not open quran.com or tafsir sites (Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari) directly, so classical tafsir wording on 12:44 and 10:64 is not quoted verbatim.

## 2. Status of the true dream: "part of prophethood", "mubashshirat", beginning of revelation, end of time

### Takeaway
The true/righteous dream is "one part of forty-six parts of prophethood" (Bukhari/Muslim; other sahih wordings give 45 and 70). After prophethood ended, only the *mubashshirat*, glad tidings through righteous dreams, remain. Revelation to the Prophet ﷺ began with true dreams that came "like the breaking of dawn". Near the end of time the believer's dream will hardly ever be false.

### Cited Findings
- **Bukhari 3** ('A'isha, Kitab Bad' al-Wahy): أَوَّلُ مَا بُدِئَ بِهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ مِنَ الْوَحْيِ الرُّؤْيَا الصَّالِحَةُ فِي النَّوْمِ، فَكَانَ لَا يَرَى رُؤْيَا إِلَّا جَاءَتْ مِثْلَ فَلَقِ الصُّبْحِ
  বাংলা: "রাসূলুল্লাহ ﷺ-এর প্রতি ওহীর সূচনা হয়েছিল ঘুমের মধ্যে সত্য (সৎ) স্বপ্নের মাধ্যমে; তিনি যে স্বপ্নই দেখতেন তা ভোরের আলোর মতো (স্পষ্ট হয়ে) বাস্তবে প্রকাশ পেত।" Repeated at Bukhari 6982 (Kitab al-Ta'bir, ch. 1). ## 3. The three types of dreams

### Takeaway
Dreams fall into three types: (1) glad tidings from Allah (ru'ya saliha); (2) frightening or grieving dreams from Shaytan (hulm); (3) a person's own thoughts and preoccupations (hadith al-nafs). This division is explicitly marfu' (the Prophet's words) in Ibn Majah 3907 (sahih). In Bukhari 7017 it appears as Ibn Sirin's words ("it used to be said"). In Muslim 2263 it appears in a marfu' wording, though one route has it as mawquf on Ibn Sirin, and scholars discuss whether it was inserted into the hadith (idraj).

### Cited Findings
- **Muslim 2263** (continuation): ... وَالرُّؤْيَا ثَلَاثَةٌ: فَرُؤْيَا الصَّالِحَةِ بُشْرَى مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَرُؤْيَا تَحْزِينٌ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ، وَرُؤْيَا مِمَّا يُحَدِّثُ الْمَرْءُ نَفْسَهُ. فَإِنْ رَأَى أَحَدُكُمْ مَا يَكْرَهُ فَلْيَقُمْ فَلْيُصَلِّ، وَلَا يُحَدِّثْ بِهَا النَّاسَ
  বাংলা: "স্বপ্ন তিন প্রকার: (১) সৎ স্বপ্ন, যা আল্লাহর পক্ষ থেকে সুসংবাদ; (২) শয়তানের পক্ষ থেকে দুঃখ-ভীতি সৃষ্টিকারী স্বপ্ন; (৩) মানুষ (জাগ্রত অবস্থায়) নিজের মনে যা ভাবে, তা থেকে (সৃষ্ট) স্বপ্ন। তোমাদের কেউ অপছন্দনীয় কিছু দেখলে সে যেন উঠে সালাত আদায় করে এবং মানুষকে তা না বলে।" — [sunnah.com Muslim 2263](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2263) [V for hadith; Arabic of this segment K]
- **Bukhari 7017**: after the marfu' part, Muhammad ibn Sirin says "وأنا أقول هذه" and "كان يُقال: الرؤيا ثلاث: حديث النفس، وتخويف الشيطان، وبشرى من الله". So in Bukhari the three-types statement is **Ibn Sirin's / an early saying, not explicitly the Prophet's**. — [amrayn Bukhari 7017](https://amrayn.com/bukhari:7017); [hadithunlocked Bukhari 7017](https://hadithunlocked.com/bukhari:7017) [V]
- The editor of al-Hakim's *al-Mustadrak* notes that Muslim (2263) also narrates it via Hammad ibn Zayd from Ayyub and Hisham from Ibn Sirin **mawquf** (without mentioning the Prophet). The marfu' version is spread in portions across Ahmad, Bukhari 7017 and Muslim 2263. — [tohed: Mustadrak 8373 note](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/mustadrak-hakim/8373/); [hadithunlocked: Hakim 8174](https://hadithunlocked.com/hakim:8174); [Bazzar 9314](https://hadithunlocked.com/bazzar:9314) [V]
- **Sunan Ibn Majah 3907** ('Awf ibn Malik; Kitab Ta'bir al-Ru'ya, ch. "الرؤيا ثلاث"), explicitly marfu': "Dreams are of three types: some are terrifying things from Shaytan meant to grieve the son of Adam; some are what a man is concerned with while awake, so he sees it in his sleep; and some are a part of the forty-six parts of prophethood." **Grading: sahih (al-Albani); also sahih per Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut and Zubair 'Ali Za'i.** Parallel in Ibn Hibban 6042. — [islamtics Ibn Majah 3907](https://islamtics.com/hadith/ibn-majah/3907/); [tohed Ibn Majah 3907](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/ibn-majah/3907); [Ibn Hibban 6042](https://hadithunlocked.com/ibnhibban:6042) [V]
- **Bukhari 6986 / Muslim 2261** (Abu Qatada): الرُّؤْيَا الصَّالِحَةُ مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحُلْمُ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ. This is the basic terminology: *ru'ya* (good, from Allah) vs *hulm* (bad, from Shaytan). — [Bukhari 6986](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6986); [Muslim 2261](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2261) [K]
- **Muslim 2268** (Jabir): a bedouin dreamt his head was cut off. The Prophet ﷺ said: لَا تُخْبِرْ بِتَلَعُّبِ الشَّيْطَانِ بِكَ فِي الْمَنَامِ ("Do not tell people of Shaytan's toying with you in your sleep"). This is an example of type 2. — [Muslim 2268](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2268) [K]

### Inferences
- For the Bengali report: cite **Ibn Majah 3907 (sahih, Albani)** as the clearest explicitly Prophetic text for the three types, with Muslim 2263 as primary support, noting the Bukhari/Ibn Sirin nuance.

### Gaps

## 4. Conditions / causes that make a person's dreams true

### Takeaway
The texts name these factors: **truthfulness in speech** (Muslim 2263); **righteousness and iman** ("من الرجل الصالح", "رؤيا المؤمن", Bukhari 6983/6988); **nearness of the end of time** (Bukhari 7017, Muslim 2263); and, as supporting means, **sleeping in wudu on the right side with the prescribed dhikr** (Bukhari 247), with an angel guarding one who sleeps in a state of purity (Ibn Hibban 1051, hasan-sahih per al-Albani). The popular claim that the truest dreams are those seen at **sahar** (pre-dawn) is in Tirmidhi 2274 and is **da'if**.

### Cited Findings
- **Truthfulness:** "وَأَصْدَقُكُمْ رُؤْيَا أَصْدَقُكُمْ حَدِيثًا": the most truthful in dreams are the most truthful in speech. — [Muslim 2263](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2263) [V]
- **Righteousness / faith:** "الرُّؤْيَا الْحَسَنَةُ مِنَ الرَّجُلِ الصَّالِحِ" (Bukhari 6983); "رُؤْيَا الْمُؤْمِنِ" (Bukhari 6988, 7017); "رُؤْيَا الْمُسْلِمِ" (Muslim 2263). — [Bukhari 6983](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6983) [K]; [Bukhari 7017](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/7017) [V]
- **End of time:** "إِذَا اقْتَرَبَ الزَّمَانُ لَمْ تَكَدْ رُؤْيَا الْمُؤْمِنِ تَكْذِبُ". — [Muslim 2263](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2263); [Bukhari 7017](https://amrayn.com/bukhari:7017) [V]
- **Wudu + right side + bedtime dua (Bukhari 247, al-Bara' ibn 'Azib; Kitab al-Wudu', ch. "فضل من بات على الوضوء"):** إِذَا أَتَيْتَ مَضْجَعَكَ فَتَوَضَّأْ وُضُوءَكَ لِلصَّلَاةِ، ثُمَّ اضْطَجِعْ عَلَى شِقِّكَ الْأَيْمَنِ... "If you die that night you die upon the fitra; and if you reach morning you attain good." Muslim 2710 is parallel. — [sunnah.com Bukhari 247](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:247); [tohed Bukhari 247](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/bukhari/247/) [V]
- **Angel with the one who sleeps in purity (Sahih Ibn Hibban 1051):** مَنْ بَاتَ طَاهِرًا بَاتَ فِي شِعَارِهِ مَلَكٌ، فَلَمْ يَسْتَيْقِظْ إِلَّا قَالَ الْمَلَكُ: اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِعَبْدِكَ فُلَانٍ، فَإِنَّهُ بَاتَ طَاهِرًا
  বাংলা: "যে ব্যক্তি পবিত্র অবস্থায় রাত কাটায়, তার পোশাকের (শরীরের সংলগ্ন কাপড়ের) মধ্যে একজন ফেরেশতা রাত কাটায়; সে যখনই জাগে, ফেরেশতা বলে: 'হে আল্লাহ! তোমার অমুক বান্দাকে ক্ষমা করো, কেননা সে পবিত্র অবস্থায় রাত কাটিয়েছে।'"
  **Grading: al-Albani "hasan sahih" (al-Silsila al-Sahiha 2539).** He also notes that attribution to Ibn 'Umar in Ibn Hibban is probably an error, the correct being Abu Hurayra. **Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut:** narrators are those of the Sahih except al-Hasan ibn Dhakwan, whom several critics weakened. Also narrated by al-Tabarani (al-Kabir, from Ibn 'Abbas) and al-Bazzar. — [hadithunlocked Ibn Hibban 1051](https://hadithunlocked.com/ibnhibban:1051); [tohed Ibn Hibban 1051](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/sahih-ibn-hibban/1051); [Tabarani 13620](https://hadithunlocked.com/tabarani:13620) [V]. *(Its number in Sahih al-Targhib was not confirmed; commonly cited as ~597/599, unverified.)*
- **Sahar time, WEAK: Tirmidhi 2274** (Abu Sa'id): أَصْدَقُ الرُّؤْيَا بِالْأَسْحَارِ ("the truest dreams are those at the pre-dawn hours"). **Da'if: al-Albani (Da'if al-Tirmidhi 2274; al-Silsila al-Da'ifa 1732; Da'if al-Jami' 887).** Defect: Ibn Lahi'a (memory confused) and Darraj Abu al-Samh from Abu al-Haytham (a weak route). Ibn 'Adi called it munkar. Shu'ayb al-Arna'ut: chain weak (takhrij Sahih Ibn Hibban). Al-Hakim graded it sahih and al-Dhahabi agreed, but al-Mubarakfuri (Tuhfat al-Ahwadhi) rejected this. — [tohed Tirmidhi 2274](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/tirmidhi/2274); [dorar](https://dorar.net/h/6LxoWMhG?sims=1); [hadithanswers](https://hadithanswers.com/dreams-seen-during-the-last-portion-of-the-night/) [V]. **⚠ FLAG AS DA'IF in the report.**
- **Self-talk dreams** (things one is preoccupied with by day) are not true dreams. — [Ibn Majah 3907](https://islamtics.com/hadith/ibn-majah/3907/) [V]

### Inferences
- In the report, give sahih conditions (truthfulness, righteousness, purity at bedtime, adhkar) priority, and present the "sahar" idea only as a weak narration. Al-Mubarakfuri notes that rational explanations exist (calm mind, empty stomach), but the hadith itself is da'if.
- Bukhari 247 does not *explicitly* mention dreams. Linking wudu at bedtime to good dreams is an inference from its virtue (dying on fitra, angelic protection) and from Ibn Hibban 1051, so present it as such.

### Gaps
- No sahih hadith was found that *explicitly* says "sleeping in wudu makes your dreams true". That is a scholarly inference.

## 5. The Prophet's ﷺ instructions on seeing good or bad dreams

### Takeaway
**Good dream:** praise Allah, take it as glad tidings, and tell it only to someone you love or a knowledgeable, wise person. **Bad dream:** (1) seek refuge in Allah from Shaytan and from the dream's evil (×3); (2) spit/blow dryly to the left (×3); (3) tell no one; (4) turn to the other side; (5) stand and pray. The Prophet ﷺ promised "it will not harm him". A dream "is on a bird's leg until it is interpreted". Lying about dreams carries a severe threat. The Prophet ﷺ routinely asked companions after Fajr whether anyone had seen a dream.

### Cited Findings

**Good dreams:**

  বাংলা: "ভালো স্বপ্ন আল্লাহর পক্ষ থেকে। তোমাদের কেউ পছন্দনীয় কিছু দেখলে সে যেন তা শুধু তাকেই বলে যাকে সে ভালোবাসে। আর অপছন্দনীয় কিছু দেখলে সে যেন এর অনিষ্ট ও শয়তানের অনিষ্ট থেকে আল্লাহর আশ্রয় চায়, তিনবার (বাম দিকে) থুথু ফেলে (থুতুবিহীন ফুঁ দেয়), এবং কাউকে তা না বলে; তাহলে তা তার কোনো ক্ষতি করবে না।"

  বাংলা: "তোমাদের কেউ ঘুমের মধ্যে ভয় পেলে সে যেন বলে: 'আমি আশ্রয় চাই আল্লাহর পরিপূর্ণ বাণীসমূহের মাধ্যমে, তাঁর ক্রোধ ও শাস্তি থেকে, তাঁর বান্দাদের অনিষ্ট থেকে, শয়তানদের কুমন্ত্রণা থেকে এবং তারা আমার কাছে উপস্থিত হওয়া থেকে'; তাহলে তা (ভয়ের বিষয়) তার ক্ষতি করবে না।"

### Inferences
- A combined checklist for bad dreams from sahih texts: ① ta'awwudh ×3 (from Shaytan and from the dream's evil) ② dry spit/blow left ×3 ③ turn sides ④ stand and pray ⑤ tell no one ⑥ (hasan) the "a'udhu bi-kalimatillah al-tammat..." dua. The promise "لَنْ تَضُرَّهُ" (it will not harm him) is sahih (Bukhari 7044, Muslim 2261).
- "Tell only one you love" (Bukhari 7044) plus "a wise/knowledgeable person" (Tirmidhi 2278) together mirror Qur'an 12:5.

### Gaps
- Some later scholars (e.g. Ibn Hajar) mention reciting Ayat al-Kursi after a bad dream, but no sahih marfu' text for this was found. Treat it as scholarly advice, not hadith.

## 6. Sleep etiquette hadith linked to good dreams, with Arabic duas and Bengali translations

### Takeaway
Authentic bedtime sunnahs: wudu, lying on the right side, and the "Allahumma aslamtu..." dua (Bukhari 247/6313, Muslim 2710); Ayat al-Kursi (Bukhari 2311, guardian until morning); the three Quls blown into the palms and wiped over the body ×3 (Bukhari 5017); and "Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya" (Bukhari 6312). The "angel with the one who sleeps in purity" (Ibn Hibban 1051) is hasan-sahih per al-Albani with some dispute. The dua "La ilaha illallah al-Wahid al-Qahhar..." on turning at night is sahih per al-Albani / hasan per Ibn Hajar, but Abu Hatim and Abu Zur'a judged the connected chain an error.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Wudu, right side, and dua: Bukhari 247 (al-Bara' ibn 'Azib); also Bukhari 6311, 6313, 6315; Muslim 2710**
> إِذَا أَتَيْتَ مَضْجَعَكَ فَتَوَضَّأْ وُضُوءَكَ لِلصَّلَاةِ، ثُمَّ اضْطَجِعْ عَلَى شِقِّكَ الْأَيْمَنِ، ثُمَّ قُلْ:
> **اللَّهُمَّ أَسْلَمْتُ وَجْهِي (وفي رواية: نَفْسِي) إِلَيْكَ، وَفَوَّضْتُ أَمْرِي إِلَيْكَ، وَأَلْجَأْتُ ظَهْرِي إِلَيْكَ، رَغْبَةً وَرَهْبَةً إِلَيْكَ، لَا مَلْجَأَ وَلَا مَنْجَا مِنْكَ إِلَّا إِلَيْكَ، اللَّهُمَّ آمَنْتُ بِكِتَابِكَ الَّذِي أَنْزَلْتَ، وَبِنَبِيِّكَ الَّذِي أَرْسَلْتَ**
> فَإِنْ مُتَّ مِنْ لَيْلَتِكَ فَأَنْتَ عَلَى الْفِطْرَةِ، وَاجْعَلْهُنَّ آخِرَ مَا تَتَكَلَّمُ بِهِ

বাংলা: "যখন তুমি বিছানায় যাবে, তখন সালাতের ওযুর মতো ওযু করবে, তারপর ডান কাতে শোবে, তারপর বলবে: 'হে আল্লাহ! আমি আমার চেহারা (অন্য বর্ণনায়: নিজেকে) তোমার কাছে সমর্পণ করলাম, আমার সব বিষয় তোমার উপর ন্যস্ত করলাম, আমার পিঠ তোমার আশ্রয়ে দিলাম, তোমার প্রতি আশা ও ভয় নিয়ে। তোমার কাছ থেকে পালানোর বা মুক্তির কোনো আশ্রয়স্থল নেই, তুমি ছাড়া। হে আল্লাহ! তুমি যে কিতাব নাযিল করেছ তার প্রতি এবং তুমি যে নবী পাঠিয়েছ তাঁর প্রতি আমি ঈমান আনলাম।' এ রাতে তুমি মারা গেলে ফিতরাতের (ইসলামের) উপর মারা যাবে। আর এ কথাগুলোকে তোমার শেষ কথা বানাও।"
Note: when al-Bara' said "wa bi-rasulika", the Prophet ﷺ corrected him: "No, wa bi-nabiyyika alladhi arsalta". Bukhari 6313 has the "aslamtu nafsi" wording. — [sunnah.com Bukhari 247](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:247); [tohed Bukhari 247](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/bukhari/247/); [tohed Bukhari 6313](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/bukhari/6313/); [tohed Bukhari 6315](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/bukhari/6315/) [V]; [Muslim 2710](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2710) [K]

**(b) Ayat al-Kursi: Bukhari 2311** (Abu Hurayra, the story of guarding the zakat dates; Bukhari gives this chain in mu'allaq form ("وقال عثمان بن الهيثم"), but it is connected elsewhere and accepted; also 3275, 5010):

**(g) Dua when tossing/turning at night ("تضوّر من الليل"):**
> كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ إِذَا تَضَوَّرَ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ قَالَ: **لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ الْوَاحِدُ الْقَهَّارُ، رَبُّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا الْعَزِيزُ الْغَفَّارُ**

বাংলা: "রাতে (ঘুমের মধ্যে) পাশ ফেরার বা অস্থির হওয়ার সময় রাসূলুল্লাহ ﷺ বলতেন: 'আল্লাহ ছাড়া কোনো ইলাহ নেই; তিনি এক, মহাপরাক্রমশালী; আসমানসমূহ, যমীন ও এ দুয়ের মধ্যবর্তী সবকিছুর রব; মহাপরাক্রান্ত, অতি ক্ষমাশীল।'"

### Inferences
- A sunnah bedtime routine that supports good dreams (all sahih unless noted): wudu → right side → Ayat al-Kursi → 3 Quls with blowing and wiping ×3 → "Allahumma aslamtu..." as the last words → "Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya". On waking frightened: the hasan "a'udhu bi-kalimatillah al-tammat..." dua plus the Muslim 2261/2262 steps. On turning at night: the "al-Wahid al-Qahhar" dhikr (disputed) or the Bukhari 1154 dhikr (sahih).
