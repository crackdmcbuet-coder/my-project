# Bangla-language and Bangla-translated books on brain fog, focus, memory, sleep, stress and brain health: availability in Bangladesh and West Bengal

> **Method note (read first):** This research was done on 2026-11-10. In this session the retailer sites (rokomari.com, wafilife.com, baatighar.com) **could not be fetched**: the hosts are outside the session's allowed network domains. So every bibliographic detail below (translator, publisher, ISBN, price, year) comes from **web-search result listings and snippets** that point to those retailer pages, not from a direct reading of the pages. Prices on Bangladeshi retailers change often and run discounted, so treat every price as a snapshot. Confirm the details on the linked page before citing them as final. To verify directly, allow rokomari.com, wafilife.com and baatighar.com in the session's network settings.

## Q1. Which popular English titles have Bangla translations (translator, publisher, where to buy)?

### Takeaway
Of the requested English titles, Bangla translations were confirmed for **Deep Work, Atomic Habits, The Power of Habit and Ikigai**, each in several competing editions from Bangladeshi publishers, plus Indian (Kolkata/Delhi) editions of Atomic Habits and Ikigai. **No Bangla translation was found** for Why We Sleep, Dopamine Nation, Stolen Focus, Digital Minimalism, Spark, Brain Food or This Is Your Brain on Food. Those titles appear to be available only in English in Bangladesh.

### Inferences
- The Bangladeshi translation market is crowded with **multiple competing editions of the same bestseller**: Deep Work has 2 or more editions, The Power of Habit 3 or more, Atomic Habits 2 or more, and Ikigai many. Translation quality and legitimacy vary. Copyright licensing could not be confirmed for any Bangladeshi edition, whereas the Indian editions (Manjul, Diamond) come from established licensed publishers.
- The **science-heavy sleep, brain-nutrition and neuroscience titles** (Why We Sleep, Brain Food, Spark, Dopamine Nation, Stolen Focus) have not been translated. The Bangla market for this topic is dominated by habit and productivity self-help, not neuroscience.
- **West Bengal buyers** can get Atomic Habits in Bangla (Manjul) and Ikigai in Bangla (Diamond) through Amazon.in, Flipkart and Oxford Bookstore. **Bangladeshi buyers** use Rokomari, Wafilife, Baatighar, PBS and Daraz.
- **অনুভূতি প্রকাশনী** appears repeatedly as a recent (2024 to 2025) publisher of these productivity translations: Deep Work and The Power of Habit.

### Gaps
- Translator and publisher for several Rokomari listings (Deep Work 436504/536290, Atomic Habits 345784/556783, Ikigai 302451 and others) could not be read, because Rokomari was not fetchable in this session.
- None of the publishers the assignment named as examples (Adarsha, Anupam, Rodela, Shabdoshilpo, Boiwala, Onnoprokash, Bookish) appeared in the results for these titles. That does not prove they have no such title; it was simply not found.
- I could not confirm whether any Indian (Kolkata) publisher has translated Deep Work or The Power of Habit into Bangla.
- Boitoi, Prothoma and Boighor were not reached. No ebook availability was checked beyond the Rokomari Ikigai ebook and Apple Books.

## Q2. Are there Bangla-original books on মনোযোগ, স্মৃতিশক্তি, মানসিক ক্লান্তি, ঘুম, ব্রেন হেলথ, স্ট্রেস (for example by Bangladeshi or Indian Bengali doctors or psychologists)?

### Takeaway
Bangla-original books on **memory and the brain** exist, including at least two by doctors. I could not verify, with a source, any Bangla-original book specifically on **brain fog, mental fatigue, sleep or stress** by a named doctor or psychologist. Bangla web content on those topics is mostly newspaper and blog articles.

### Inferences
- Bangla-original writing on memory exists, but some of it is older and out of print, such as Dr. Shyamal Chakraborty's book. A modern, evidence-based Bangla-original book on brain fog, sleep science or burnout appears to be a **gap in the market**.
- For a Bangla-reading audience, the most practical current options are translated productivity and habit books (Q1) plus the few memory titles above. For sleep and stress specifically, readers would likely need English editions.

### Gaps
- Publishers and years for the three Bangla-original memory books above were not visible, because Rokomari could not be fetched.
- I found no verifiable Bangla-original book specifically on ঘুম (sleep), মানসিক ক্লান্তি (mental fatigue), ব্রেন ফগ or স্ট্রেস by a Bangladeshi psychiatrist or psychologist. Searches returned only articles and category pages. Such books may exist under titles my queries did not match.
- West Bengal (Kolkata) Bangla-original health publishers, such as those publishing popular health columns as books, were not covered.
- No Goodreads Bengali listing was successfully retrieved.

## Q3. Availability and price of English editions in Bangladesh; relevant Islamic or self-development Bangla books

### Takeaway
English editions of the science titles are sold in Bangladesh, but at a high price relative to Bangla editions. For example, Why We Sleep is about ৳1,080 to ৳1,398, against about ৳139 to ৳400 for Bangla translations. No Islamic Bangla book with clear, verified relevance to brain fog or focus was found, so none is recommended.

### Cited Findings
- **Why We Sleep (English paperback)** on Rokomari: ৳1,398, with cash on delivery across Bangladesh. — [Rokomari 340756](https://www.rokomari.com/book/340756/why-we-sleep)
- **Why We Sleep (Penguin paperback, ISBN 9780141983769)** at Pathak Shamabesh: listed ৳1,080. The page also shows other books' prices, so this should be verified. Delivery outside Dhaka starts from ৳150. — [Pathak Shamabesh](https://pathakshamabesh.com/shop/9780141983769-1-why-we-sleep-the-new-science-of-sleep-and-dreams-10823)
- Why We Sleep is also listed by Bangladeshi stores Wardah Books and MBA Bookstore. Prices were not captured. — [Wardah Books](https://wardahbooks.com/products/why-we-sleep); [MBA Bookstore](https://mbabookstore.com/product/why-we-sleep-by-matthew-walker/)
- For comparison, the UK paperback is £12.99 at Waterstones. — [Waterstones](https://www.waterstones.com/book/9780141983769)
- Bangla translations are far cheaper: Deep Work ৳139 to ৳318, Atomic Habits about ৳339, Ikigai ৳129 to ৳199 (see the Q1 citations). Atomic Habits in Bengali costs ₹289 in India. — [Zepto](https://www.zepto.com/pn/atomic-habits-bengali/pvid/85bb8f3d-2dcd-4550-b650-f82e07827119)
- Bikroy hosts secondhand and resale classifieds for Atomic Habits in Bangladesh. — [Bikroy](https://bikroy.com/bn/books-and-games/atomic-habits)

### Inferences
- An English paperback of a neuroscience title costs roughly 3 to 10 times as much as a Bangla translation of a productivity title in Bangladesh. That price gap probably explains why translated habit and productivity books dominate.
- Many "Bangla PDF" piracy sites (pdfpoka, porageducation, boimate, allbanglaboi) show up for these titles. They should not be recommended, and they signal strong demand.

### Gaps
- English-edition prices in Bangladesh were not captured for Deep Work, Atomic Habits, Stolen Focus, Dopamine Nation, Digital Minimalism, Spark, Brain Food or This Is Your Brain on Food, because the retailer sites could not be fetched.
- Islamic Bangla books: no targeted search found a title with verifiable relevance to focus, memory, sleep or stress, so none is listed.
- Amazon.in prices for the English editions (relevant to West Bengal) were not collected.
